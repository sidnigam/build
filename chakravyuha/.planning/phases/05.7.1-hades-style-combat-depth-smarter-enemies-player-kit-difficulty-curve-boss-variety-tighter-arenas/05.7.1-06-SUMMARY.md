# Phase 05.7.1 Plan 06: Mini-Bosses Summary

**Four asura and rakshasa champions now hold a fixed mid-biome room: Subahu, Mahiravana, Alambusha and Maricha. Each is a `MiniBoss extends Boss` with its own data-driven projectile and status moveset and enrages at 40% HP. Each is reached through a single ⚔ door and pays out 60 mudra plus a Rare-or-better boon. None of them ever touches the biome-boss path. Biomes are now shorter and denser, at 4/5/5/3 rooms.**

## Accomplishments

### Boss hooks (behaviour-preserving refactor)
- `Boss` has new overridable hooks: `moveTable(phase)`, `moveDefBase(id)`, `phaseAt()`, `phaseTitle()` and `customInitWind/customWindTelegraph/customActive`. Its constructor also takes an optional `def`.
- `pickMove`, `moveDef` and the phase trigger now go through these hooks, so plan 07 can rewrite `pickMove` once and mini-bosses get chains and no-repeat automatically.
- New generic `pattern` move kind:
  - During the wind, `PROJ.planPattern` supplies the marks.
  - Once armed, `_runPattern` fires `volleys` × `volleyGap` through the guarded `Boss.shoot`, merging `pat.extra` and `pat.status`.
  - In phase 2, `'around' | 'radial' | 'player'` patterns get +1 projectile.
- `planPattern` `'line'` now honours `count`/`spread`, producing one beam and one line mark per angle.
- Refactor proof: the `balance --quick` aggregate stayed bit-identical, and smoke kept its 974 ok checks with every boss check unchanged.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] 'around' bombs fused on the caster**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (goldBombs test)
- **Issue:** `planPattern` 'around' shots carry only `extra.{tx, ty}` with no velocity. The bomb behaviour moves by `vx/vy`, so Maricha's bombs sat on Maricha and fused there while the telegraph circles showed elsewhere. Without the fix, 0 of 3 bombs reached a mark.
- **Fix:** `_runPattern` throws bomb-behaviour shots that have a target at `(target - origin) / def.travel`. They now land on their marks, 3 of 3.
- **Files modified:** js/entities.js
- **Commit:** 41a8342

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Method placement for the smoke static scan**
- **Issue:** The smoke static scan strips the first `shoot(` method body inside `class Boss`. Putting `_runPattern` (which calls `this.shoot(`) before `shoot()` would have made the scan miss the real `shoot()` body and flag its `enemyShot(` call.
- **Fix:** `patternFor`/`_runPattern` are placed after `shoot()`. The scan is unchanged.
- **Commit:** f343f8b

**3. [Rule 2 - Correctness] Small robustness additions**
- `startMove` clears `patPlan`, so a pattern interrupted by the enrage beat cannot leak into the next move.
- The smoke `simulateRun` boss clock resets when the boss-flagged target changes. Without that, a mini killed right before a no-combat room (sarovar/kubera) could shorten the next boss's 20 s act window.
- The mini reward also plays the `coin` sfx, as the mudra reward does.
- **Commits:** f343f8b, 8d3cfdf

**Total deviations:** 1 bug, 1 blocking placement issue, plus small correctness additions. No architectural changes.
