**Create `tools/art/shot_stats.py SHOT.png... [--json OUT]`.**
- For each screenshot, sample the floor in the central 60% region. Exclude pixels that look like entities/telegraphs: saturation > 0.55 or luma > 0.65, which are HUD/character/telegraph highlights; report the excluded fraction.
- Compute luma mean/std and sat mean over the rest. Flag a shot FAIL if mean > 0.30, std > 0.12 or sat_mean > 0.30 (D-08b).
- For `-combat.png` shots, also report the peak telegraph contrast: the max luma difference between pixels matching the hazard colour hue (±12°) and the median floor luma. Flag WARN if < 0.25.
- Print a table and exit non-zero on any FAIL.

**Loop, at most about 4 iterations per biome.**
1. Run `node tools/art/env_shots.mjs $SCRATCH/env-shots-05` for all biomes, then `shot_stats.py` over all PNGs.
2. Build one review grid per biome (`--cell 480 --cols 3`: nw/center/se + boss + combat + one other layout) and Read it. Check against the D-08 guardrails and the Hades target:
   - floor detail visible but calm;
   - repetition broken by overlay/decals/props;
   - walls frame without looking like a box outline;
   - props sit at the margins at believable scale next to the ~52px player (tune worldH);
   - column/gate proportions right;
   - lights read as warm/cold pools, not glare;
   - Ranabhoomi shafts and Vaitarani mist subtle;
   - pillar skins fade when the player is behind;
   - chariot_wreck/dhvaja/mooring_post skins match their collision;
   - water bands scroll with the current;
   - vent grates sit under the telegraphs;
   - telegraphs, doors and enemies pop.
3. Adjust the manifest tuning fields first (tileScale, macroAlpha, worldH, grade.*, light alphas/radii), and ENV.CFG only for cross-biome issues. If a single asset is unfixable by tuning (for example a prop that reads too bright even when graded), set its `weight: 0` to drop it from placement and note it. Keep ≥ 6 props per biome. Regenerating art is out of scope here.
4. Repeat until all shots pass shot_stats with no FAIL, there are no WARNs on combat shots, and the visual review is clean.

Record the before/after tuning values and the final shot_stats table in the SUMMARY. The cultural check applies to what is visible in the screenshots too: nothing sacred placed where it is walked on, and no gore.
