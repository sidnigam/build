## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Bot policy could not leave wall pockets, so seeds timed out**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (first full-sim runs)
- **Issue:** With the plan's straight-line movement and a fixed 0.6 s strafe, the bot got stuck permanently in template layouts that form wall crosses and pockets (for example naraka_forge, patala_halls). Results: 4/30 zero-meta timeouts, 12-15/30 max-meta timeouts, and bow runs stuck shooting at a target behind a wall edge. Those numbers measure the bot, not the game.
- **Fix:** Added grid-BFS navigation (32 px cells, string-pulled waypoint, path re-planned every 0.25 s) whenever line of sight to the goal is blocked. The stuck strafe now escalates: each consecutive stuck window strafes longer (0.6 s × k, capped at 3 s) and alternates sides. The bow bot advances when line of sight is blocked (8 px pad, wider than the 5 px arrow radius). The ringMark dodge and the band short-axis escape were also added to the threat detector. Result: 0 timeouts for every weapon and meta mode.
- **Files modified:** test/balance.js
- **Commit:** ccff068 (all before the fixture was captured)

**2. [Rule 2 - Correctness] The telegraph test checks shape, not just colour**
- **Found during:** Task 3 RED
- **Issue:** The old sprite path drew the ring telegraph as `arc(x, y, undefined)` and then called fill/stroke in red, so a colour-only check would wrongly pass the spiral/quake ring. The plan says the ring must fail before the fix.
- **Fix:** The test also requires finite arc args, a ring arc at radius (r0+r1)/2, and a cone arc spanning `tg.arc`. RED then failed exactly on summon, teleport, lamps, crossBeams, ledger, noose, spiral/quake ring and maw/roar/danda cone.
- **Commit:** 20de533

### Small primitive-path position shifts (allowed by the plan, ≤ 4 px or same anchor)

- Regular-enemy stun glyph: `y - r - 10` became `headY - 12` = `y - r - 12` (2 px higher).
- The maya ring on the primitive path is now an ellipse at the feet (`y + r`, radius r+4, 0.4 vertical squash), matching the sprite path. Before, it was a circle of radius r+4 around the centre.
- Boss zzz and burn pips now draw after the body and eyes (same coordinates). Before, the zzz was drawn before the eyes.
- Regular-enemy telegraphs on the sprite path keep full size (frac 1), as before. Only bosses use fill-progress.
