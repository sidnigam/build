  <action>
**1. `framing.md`** (one page, plain language, for a reviewer who may not game). It covers:
- **The premise.** Abhimanyu, the Chakravyuha, the afterlife climb through Yama's realm.
- **The hard rule.** Gods are allies and boon-givers only. Opponents are asuras and rakshasas only.
- **The Yama decision.** Yama Dharmaraja is a judge. The final encounter is a **Trial of Dharma** in which he weighs Abhimanyu. Passing it is "passing judgment", never a death. Explain the rationale (05.7 UD-1, D-02 to D-07), and say exactly what the mechanics still show: a combat test.
- **Iconography choices**, with a per-god and per-boss table of attributes, for example Yama's danda/pasha/buffalo motif and Durga's weapons.
- **How the art was made.** AI-generated, locally, with human curation, plus the regeneration history.
- **What we are asking.** Approve, revise, or hard no, per item, with free-text notes. Reviewers may also flag anything not listed.

**2. `flagged-items.json`.** An array of `{id, title, asset_paths[], concern, action_taken, status, question_for_reviewer}` for the 12 items in `<interfaces>`.

**3. `sign-off-form.md`.** The template the packet renders:
- reviewer name, background (textual/academic, devotional practice, both, other), date, and whether they consent to be credited
- per flagged item, and per review section (Art: bosses, gods, enemies, environments, store art; Narrative: dialogue, lore, boss text, achievements, store copy), the verdict `[ ] Approved  [ ] Needs revision  [ ] Hard no` plus a note
- an overall verdict
- the stated rule: "a single Hard no blocks launch until resolved"

**4. `outreach.md`:**
- **Sourcing plan.** Three routes from 07-RESEARCH:
  - academic: South Asian or Religious Studies faculty
  - community: Hindu cultural centers and organizations such as HAF, approached with transparent intent
  - professional: sensitivity-reading consultants

  Aim for diversity of perspective, with at least one reviewer with Mahabharata textual depth and one with a devotional-practice perspective. Vet for depth, not identity alone.
- **A short, respectful email draft.** Say who you are, what the game is, what you are asking and how long it takes (about 2-3 hours with the packet), the offered honorarium (a placeholder: $50-150, the user decides), the credit option, and a deadline placeholder. Note that the packet is attached and no gameplay is required.
- **A follow-up template.**
- **A tracking table:** contacted, replied, packet sent, form received.
