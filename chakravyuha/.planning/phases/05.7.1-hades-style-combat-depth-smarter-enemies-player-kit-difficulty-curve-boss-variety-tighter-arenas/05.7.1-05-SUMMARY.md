# Phase 05.7.1 Plan 05: Elite Modifiers and 8 Signature Enemies Summary

**Elites now appear in every biome. Any roster type can be bought as an elite at 1.8× cost, with 1-2 visible modifiers: Armored, Hasty, Vampiric, Splitting, Shielded, Explosive, Warded and Rallying. Each modifier has an aura ring, a name tag and a working effect. The plan also adds 8 new asura, rakshasa, pishacha and naga enemies, 2 per biome. All of them run through the plan-04 token/slot/telegraph helpers, and the sims log zero ENEMY_TG violations.**

## Accomplishments

### Elites (D-07, COMBAT-05)

- **The budget bug is fixed.** composeWave buys elites from the whole biome roster at `cost × eliteMult`. Each elite gets `rollEliteMods(bi)`: 1 distinct mod in Naraka and Patala, 2 in Ranabhoomi and Vaitarani.
- **Elites per room, from the smoke info line:**

  | Biome | Before | After |
  |-------|--------|-------|
  | Naraka | 0.00 | 4.60 |
  | Patala | 0.00 | 4.00 |
  | Ranabhoomi | 10.92 | 4.00 |
  | Vaitarani | 4.00 | 6.09 |

  The old Ranabhoomi figure of 10.92 counted every unit costing 3 or more as an "elite". Naraka room 1 stays at 0.00.
- **Elite stats:** hp ×1.6, dmg ×1.15, r ×1.1, and the hp bar always shows.
- **What each modifier does:**

  | Modifier | Effect |
  |----------|--------|
  | Armored | Takes 50% damage and has no knockback until an armor bar (60% of max hp) breaks. |
  | Shielded | Blocks the first 2 non-burn hits; each block shows "BLOCK". |
  | Hasty | Moves 1.3× faster; post-attack cooldowns ×0.75. Wind-up times do not change. |
  | Vampiric | Heals 30% of the damage its landed melee or projectile hits deal. On death, heals allies within 180 px by 25% of their max hp. |
  | Splitting | On death, spawns 2 weak copies, but never past `maxAlive`. |
  | Explosive | On death, leaves a 0.6 s fused mortar with r 90. It only damages when it resolves. |
  | Warded | For 3 s out of every 8 s, turns player attack arrows back as a 0.5× needle. Casts and specials pass through. |
  | Rallying | Allies within 220 px deal ×1.2 damage through `dmgMul()`, which `fire()` and `hurtPlayer()` apply. |

- **Overlay:** drawn in the shared `drawOverlays`, so both the primitive and sprite paths show it:
  - aura ring in the first mod's colour, plus a second ring for a second mod
  - grey armor bar
  - blue shield pips
  - violet hex ring while the ward is up
  - Georgia 10px name tag, e.g. "Armored · Warded"

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The plan's eliteShare start values give no elites in 3 of 4 last rooms**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (elites suite)
- **Issue:** At `cost × 1.8`, the elite budget slices from `[0.1, 0.25] / [0.15, 0.35] / [0.25, 0.5]` cannot afford any roster type in the last rooms of Naraka, Patala and Ranabhoomi. Mean elites per wave was 0.00, against a required ≥ 1.
- **Fix:** Raised only the last-room eliteShare values, as the plan's tuning rule allows: naraka 0.35, patala 0.5, ranabhoomi 0.6 (vaitarani unchanged at 0.5). Both smoke assertions hold: ranabhoomi last room 4.00 ≥ 2, naraka room 1 0.00 < 0.5.
- **Commit:** 996b0c4

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] The javelineer could not hold 420-600 px under the plan-04 view-band pull**
- **Issue:** Plan-04 ranged slots are pulled inside the view band, which is about 225 px vertically. That would park the "sniper" at 225 px.
- **Fix:** Added a `def.longRange` flag. The javelineer's slot sits mid-band, and its throws are mostly long shots with the +0.3 s tell. Test result: 100% of idle frames fall in [380, 640].
- **Commit:** adbebf5

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] Token reclaim during the jala-rakshasa dive**
- **Issue:** `ENEMY_AI.tick` reclaims tokens from holders that have no windKind. A dive has none.
- **Fix:** Added `e.holdToken`, which `endAttack` clears.
- **Commit:** adbebf5

**4. [Rule 2 - Correctness] Swarm slot hogging and the support token pool**
- Pishachas skip ring slots (`NO_SLOT_AI`).
- Mayavi and Brahmarakshasa attacks use the 'ranged' pool. The support pool has 1 token and would have serialised every support unit.
- **Commits:** 65df6ba, adbebf5

**5. [Design detail]**
- `pack` also applies to fodder picks.
- Snares deal 0.5 × dmg so the root rides on a landed hit.
- The javelineer beam's hit width matches its 14 px telegraph.
- The brahmarakshasa heal retries every 0.5 s when no ally is wounded.
- Enemies are knocked back only when `canKnock` allows. The smash and gada-slam stuns still apply to armored elites.

**Total deviations:** 3 auto-fixed bugs, 1 correctness group and some design details. No architectural changes.
