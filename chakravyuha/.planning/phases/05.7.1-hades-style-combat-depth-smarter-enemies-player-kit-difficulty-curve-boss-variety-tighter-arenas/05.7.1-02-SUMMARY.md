# Phase 05.7.1 Plan 02: Player Statuses and Data-Driven Projectile Families Summary

**This plan adds a player status system (stun with a 0.6 s cap, diminishing returns and 1.2 s immunity, plus slow, root, burn, poison, knock and pull). Statuses apply only when a hit actually lands. It also adds 13 data-driven enemy projectile entries across 7 behaviours, with a cap, a per-owner wisp limit, ground pools and a pattern planner. Today's generic bolt is unchanged: the balance baseline matches bit-for-bit.**

## Accomplishments

- Hit-landed contract: dash, post-hit i-frames, god mode and the Lila dodge now block both the damage and the status.
- 30 s stun barrage (a stun every 0.25 s): the player is stunned **11.0%** of the time (limit 25%).
- Stun blocks movement, attack, bow draw, dash, cast and special. The guards sit inside the Player methods, so keydown and pad edges are covered too. Dashing breaks a root.
- Poison and burn ticks never grant i-frames. A poisoned player can still be hit.
- On-player status reads on both render paths. A HUD status row sits on the HP-bar line, right of the defiance lotuses: icon, remaining-time bar and stack count.
- Projectile families: needle volley, big slow orb (knockback), homing wisp (slow), fused splitting bomb, tell-then-fire beam, lobbed mortar with a ground circle, boomerang chakram, expanding ring with an optional dash gap. Status variants: venom (poison), ember (burn), coil (root), thunder (stun). Each has its own colour, draw, trail, hit VFX and sfx.
- `node test/balance.js --expect-baseline` passes, and the `--full` 30-seed aggregate equals the fixture exactly (85.507 dmg/min, 12.55 s room, 94.608 s run).

## API as shipped (later plans import this)

### DATA

- `DATA.STATUS[type]` with `type` in `stun | slow | root | burn | poison | knock | pull`. Each entry has `color` (#rrggbb), plus `icon` and `name` for the HUD types. Tunables: `stun.max 0.6`, `stun.immunity 1.2`, `stun.drWindow 4`, `stun.drFactor 0.5`, `stun.immuneSlowF 0.7`, `root.max 1.5`, `burn.maxStacks 3`, `poison.maxStacks 5`, `burn.tick 0.5` (the tick shared by both DoTs), `knock.decay 0.0001` (velocity multiplier per second).
- `DATA.PROJECTILES[fam]`: `{ behavior, draw, r, speed, life, color, trail, hitFx, sfx, destroyable, status?, ...behaviour params }`. Families: `bolt, needle, orb, wisp, bomb, beam, mortar, chakram, ring, venom, ember, coil, thunder`. Behaviour params: wisp `turn, perShooterCap`; bomb `travel, fuse, splits, splitFam, splitDmg`; beam `tell, active, len, width`; mortar `flight` (here `r` is the blast radius, 70); chakram `out, back`; ring `maxR, width, prePulse` (here `r` is the start radius).

### STATUS (js/combat.js)

- `STATUS.init(P)`. Sets `P.st = { stunT, stunMax, stunImmT, stunHist, slowT, slowF, slowMax, rootT, rootMax, burns: [{dps, t, max}], poison: [...], dotAcc, burnAcc, poisonAcc, kx, ky, fxT, clock }`. Called in the Player constructor.
- `STATUS.apply(P, spec | spec[], fromX, fromY)`. `spec` is one of:
  - `{type:'stun', dur}`
  - `{type:'slow', f, dur}`
  - `{type:'root', dur}`
  - `{type:'burn'|'poison', dps, dur}`
  - `{type:'knock'|'pull', force}`
  - An array containing a stun drops its knock/pull entries.
  - A stun during immunity becomes `slow 0.7` for the same duration.
  - Knock/pull with no source point uses `P.aim + π`.
- `STATUS.update(P, dt)`: called by `Player.update` right after the timers block.
- Queries:
  - `STATUS.stunned(P)`, `STATUS.rooted(P)`, `STATUS.slowFactor(P)`.
  - `STATUS.active(P)` returns `[{type, t, max, stacks}]` in the order stun, root, slow, burn, poison.
  - `STATUS.clear(P)`.
- `drawPlayerStatus(ctx, P, headY, feetY)` draws the reads on the player:
  - stun: 3 orbiting ✦
  - root: brown ellipse at the feet
  - slow: blue ellipse
  - burn: ring
  - poison: green pips
  - With screenFlash off it uses a constant 0.6 alpha.

### Player (js/entities.js)

- `takeDamage(amount, fromX, fromY, opts) -> boolean`. Returns `false` for i-frames, dash, god mode or a Lila dodge. If `opts.status` is set, it is applied after the damage and duo effects, before the death check.
- `tickDamage(amount, type)`: no i-frames, hitstop, flash, shake or kick. It applies the dur_kavacha reduction, shows dmgText in the status colour and plays the burn or poison sfx.
- `moveSpeed()` uses `min(hazardSlowF, STATUS.slowFactor)`.
- `tryDash/tryCast/trySpecial/meleeAttack/updateBow` return early while stunned. `tryDash` clears `st.rootT`.

### PROJ (js/combat.js)

- `PROJ.CAP = 260`, `PROJ.HARD_CAP = 300`, `PROJ.POOL_GRACE = 0.5`, `PROJ.POOL_TICK = 0.5`.
- `PROJ.spawn(fam, { x, y, vx, vy, dmg, color?, r?, life?, status?, owner?, extra? }) -> p | null`
  - `extra` is spread onto the projectile last. Keys: `tx, ty, a, tell, len, ox, oy, gapA, gapArc, speed, pool: {status, r, dur}, owner, status, splitA`.
  - Returns null when the owner's wisp cap is full, or when the hard cap is full of non-destroyable projectiles.
  - The spawn sfx is rate-limited to once per 0.07 s per sound.
  - Unknown families fall back to `bolt`.
- `PROJ.destroyable(p)`, `PROJ.def(p)`, `PROJ.addPool(x, y, {status, r, dur, color?})`.
- `PROJ.BEHAVIORS`: `straight, homing, bomb, beam, mortar, chakram, ring`. Each is `{ update(p, dt, def), hits(p, P, def), point?, ownFx?, onContact?, from?, resolve? }`.
- `PROJ.updateAll(dt)`: called by `GAME.update`. It also runs `PROJ.updatePools(dt)`.
- `PROJ.drawGround(ctx)`: called by renderBackground after dangerZones. Draws mortar circles, ring pre-pulses and pools.
- `PROJ.drawAll(ctx)`: called by renderEffects.
- `PROJ.DRAW[kind](ctx, p, def)`.
- `PROJ.planPattern(src, { fam, count=1, spread=0, aim, dmgMul=1, radius=120, offset, tell, gapArc, speed, status }, target) -> { marks, shots }`
  - Values for `aim`: `'player' | 'radial' | 'around' | 'line' | 'self'`.
  - Each shot is `{ fam, x, y, vx?, vy?, extra?, dmgMul, status? }`. Marks use the drawTelegraph types `line`, `circle` and `ringMark`.
  - Fire a shot with `PROJ.spawn(s.fam, { x: s.x, y: s.y, vx: s.vx, vy: s.vy, dmg: base * s.dmgMul, status: s.status, owner, extra: s.extra })` or through a guarded `Boss.shoot`.
- `enemyShot(x, y, vx, vy, dmg, color, r = 6, fam = 'bolt', extra)` returns the projectile. `Boss.shoot(vx, vy, dmg, r = 7, x, y, fam = 'bolt', extra)` returns the projectile and sets `owner: this` for non-bolt families or whenever `extra` is given.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The stun DR clock is status-local, not GAME.runTime**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (designing the barrage test)
- **Issue:** `GAME.runTime` only advances inside `GAME.update`, and not during hitstop. Code or tests that drive `P.update` directly would never age the DR history, and hitstop frames would count differently.
- **Fix:** `P.st.clock` advances in `STATUS.update`. It is the same sim time in normal play.
- **Commit:** 70b341b

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Legacy bolt hits keep the no-source `takeDamage(p.dmg)` call**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The plan's generic `P.takeDamage(p.dmg, p.x, p.y, ...)` would add a `CAMERA.kick` on every bolt hit. That changes behaviour, which the plan forbids for bolts.
- **Fix:** A bolt with no status uses exactly today's call. Every other family passes its source point. Beams pass their origin, mortars their target and rings their centre.
- **Commit:** 06fe8cd

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] Splicing during iteration under the cap**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** A bomb splitting while the list is at the cap would splice `GAME.eprojectiles` in the middle of `updateAll`, which shifts indices.
- **Fix:** Inside `updateAll` (`PROJ._busy`), cap eviction sets `t = 0` and the end-of-frame filter compacts the list. Outside `updateAll` the list is spliced in place, so `length <= CAP` holds immediately.
- **Commit:** 06fe8cd

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug] DoT totals are exact at expiry**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** A plain 0.5 s tick timer can lose the last tick when a stack expires on the same frame. Floating-point frame accumulation makes this happen, so 4 dps for 2 s would give 6 instead of 8.
- **Fix:** Damage accrues as dps × active time each frame and is paid every tick, with the remainder paid on expiry.
- **Commit:** 70b341b

**5. [Test fix] Entry count and beam-tell boundary in proj.test.js**
- The plan text says "14 entries / 10 families", but its own data block has 13 rows (bolt + 8 new + 4 status variants). The acceptance grep also says `>= 13`. The test now checks for 13.
- The first beam-tell check compared pre-update age, which flags the frame that crosses into the active window. It now compares post-update age.
- **Commit:** 06fe8cd

**6. [Rule 2 - Missing] Small robustness additions**
- Pools respect god mode, so "god mode blocks everything" holds for pools too.
- Beams and mortars extend their own life to cover tell+active or flight when a pattern overrides those values.
- A bomb that hits a wall stops and fuses where it is instead of vanishing.
- `PROJ._hexA` falls back to the family colour when a caller passes a non-hex colour, so gradient `addColorStop` never throws (the harness models the browser behaviour that throws).
