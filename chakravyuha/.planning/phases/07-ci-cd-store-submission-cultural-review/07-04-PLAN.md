  <name>Task 1: Curated screenshot capture (6+ scenes at 1920x1080)</name>
  <files>tools/store/capture-screenshots.mjs, store/screens.json</files>
  <action>
**1. `store/screens.json`** lists at least 8 candidate scenes as data: `{id, title, biome, setup}`. `setup` names a scripted step. Candidates:
- `naraka-combat`
- `patala-combat`
- `ranabhoomi-kumbhakarna`: the boss fight, mid-fight
- `vaitarani-combat`
- `yama-trial-intro`: 'THE TRIAL' label visible
- `boon-panel-<god>`: Hanuman or Krishna, with portrait and dialogue, framed as an ally offering a boon
- `astra-cast`: VFX
- `dharma-hub`: the Path of Dharma shrine
- `kubera-shop`

**2. Build `tools/store/capture-screenshots.mjs`** on the god_panel_shots/boss_shots pattern. It starts and stops its own `python3 -m http.server 8099` and Chrome (CDP 9339).
- Usage: `node tools/store/capture-screenshots.mjs [--out dist/store/screenshots] [--only id,...] [--gpu]`.
- Viewport: 1920x1080 at dpr 1, with `?gfx=high`.
- For each scene:
  - Seed or force a readable composition: make the player invulnerable (god-mode HP as in `--stress`), and top up enemies to a lively but readable count for combat scenes.
  - Hide any debug or dev overlay.
  - Wait for the textures to be ready, plus 1.5 s settle.
  - Screenshot `NN-<id>.png`.
- Write `manifest.json` with `{id, title, file, width, height, exceptions:[], meanLuma, stdevLuma}`, computed by sharp `stats()`.
- **Fail** (exit 1) if any shot is not exactly 1920x1080, any page exception fired, stdevLuma is below a blank-frame threshold, or fewer than 6 scenes succeeded.
- No HUD debug text, FPS counters or dev labels may be visible. Grep for and disable any `?debug` overlays.
  </action>

  </verify>
  <done>

    - The manifest is written.
  </done>
