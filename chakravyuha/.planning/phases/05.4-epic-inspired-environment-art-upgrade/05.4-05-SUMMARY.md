## What the first screenshots showed

- The boss/biome title cards covered the arena centre in the boss and combat shots.
- North-wall columns and the west gate were drawn at native frame size (~450px against a 52px player).

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] ENV rebuilt the whole room every frame, and the manifest grade was never applied**
- **Issue:** `sync()` set `_roomKey`/`_plan`, then called `build()`, whose `clear()` reset both to null. The next frame saw a "new room", so the room was re-laid-out and rebuilt every frame with a fresh `Math.random()` seed. This had three effects:
  - props re-rolled every frame and the gates were rebuilt every frame;
  - `ENV.grade()` always returned its defaults, so no manifest `floorTint`/`floorDarken`/`vignette` ever took effect;
  - the occlusion alpha lerp reset every frame.
- **Fix:** assign `_plan`/`_roomKey` after `build()` (and re-attach `_plan` inside `build`), then force a cull pass.
- **Files:** js/environment.js. **Commit:** 509c07f

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Columns and gates drawn at native frame size**
- **Issue:** column sprites used `scale: 1` (448px frames against a 150px worldH). Gate sprites ignored their 80x140 AABB entirely.
- **Fix:** columns now scale from worldH, and their AABB comes from the scaled frame. Gates are rotated side doorways fitted to the side pad.
- **Commit:** 509c07f

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] Occlusion fade could never trigger**
- **Issue:** `occluded()` tested against the pillar's collision footprint, where no actor can stand. Culling also used that footprint, so tall skins could be culled while their tops were still on screen.
- **Fix:** added a `vis` sprite rect to pillar and wall skins, used for occlusion and culling. `occluded()` falls back to `aabb`, so the existing smoke case is unchanged.
- **Commit:** 509c07f

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug] D-05a/D-07 not implemented as specified**
- **Issue:** north props were placed anywhere on the wall face instead of in the [arena.y-14, arena.y-2] base band. Manifest `light` blocks were never emitted. Pit rim decals had `frame: null`, so they never drew.
- **Fix:** north base band, `_frameLights()`, and real decal frames for the pit rims.
- **Commit:** 509c07f

**5. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] D-15 double-light hand-off with 05.6 GFX**
- **Issue:** once ENV emits prop light pools, GFX.envPropLights() would light the same props a second time at medium/high.
- **Fix:** ENV hides its entity-prop pools while `GFX.tier !== 'low'` and restores them on a drop to low. gfx.js is untouched.
- **Commit:** 509c07f

**6. [Rule 3 - Blocking / tooling] env_shots.mjs could not produce a valid review**
- **Issue:** the title-card overlays covered the arena, the combat telegraph was often off-screen, and random seeds made before/after comparisons meaningless.
- **Fix:** hide overlays before capture, frame the forced telegraph in the left third, add `ENV.CFG.FIXED_SEED` (5405, or `--random`), add `--gfx TIER`, and record the GFX tier in stats.json.
- **Commit:** 509c07f

**7. [Rule 1 - Metric correctness] shot_stats telegraph metric**
- **Issue:** HUD colours share the hazard hues. Vaitarani's surge warning is drawn white, not #9fb8ff.
- **Fix:** exclude the HUD bands, match Vaitarani achromatically, and require a telegraph *area* (≥150 px at ≥0.25 above the floor) on top of the peak contrast.
- **Commit:** 509c07f

**8. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Smoke guardrail floorTint ∈ [0.3,0.8] conflicted with the tuned floorTint 0.2**
- **Fix:** added `grade.floorWash` (game.js renderBackground region, ENV path only; the legacy path is byte-identical), set floorTint to 0.3 with calmer wash colours, and re-ran the matrix.
- **Commit:** 5d29d91

**9. [Scope note] Smoke "every env-* entry has cultural_check and ground_safe"**
- **Issue:** the 4 env-atlas entries are aggregates without these fields, and the plan says not to change asset entries.
- **Fix:** the assertion covers the 68 env-tile/env-prop entries. All 72 env entries are asserted provisional.
- **Commit:** d80e872
