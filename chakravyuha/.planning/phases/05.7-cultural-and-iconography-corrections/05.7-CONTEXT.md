# Phase 05.7: Cultural and Iconography Corrections — Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-19 (user decisions captured by the orchestrator)
**Status:** Ready for planning
**Hard project rule served:** CLAUDE.md says gods are allies only and never enemies, and that the game must show mythological reverence. Asuras and rakshasas are the only opponents.

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

This phase corrects cultural and iconography problems that were flagged during review (STATE.md Blockers/Concerns, 2026-10-17). It has two parts.

1. **Text and framing (05.7-01, no GPU).** Every player-facing mention of Yama treats the final encounter as **the Trial of Dharma**. Yama is weighing Abhimanyu. He is not a hostile enemy. Combat mechanics stay **unchanged**: HP, movesets, phases, damage and the victory flow are the same. A smoke "reverence lint" guards against regressions.
2. **Art regeneration (05.7-02, GPU, gated).** These assets are regenerated with the shipped Flux pipeline:
   - the Yama boss sprite
   - the Kumbhakarna boss sprite
   - the Agni and Indra god portraits
   - the Naraka `gate` and `vent_grate` environment frames

   Each is regenerated with corrected iconography, then gets provenance, contact sheets, the disclosure update and headless screenshots.

Out of scope:
- new mechanics
- new bosses
- re-balancing
- renaming Yama
- art that was not flagged. The Naraka floor "riveted metal" note and the Patala venom-pool brightness belong to 05.4-05.
</domain>

## Implementation Decisions (locked)

### Text / framing (Plan 01)
- **D-01** Each boss gets a per-boss HUD room label, stored as data in `DATA.BOSSES[id].label`:
  - Yama: `'THE TRIAL'`
  - Tataka, Ahiravana and Kumbhakarna keep `'THE WARDEN'`. Rakshasa and asura gatekeepers are fitting "wardens".
  - `js/game.js` reads the label through a new `GAME.roomLabel()` helper. It does not use the hard-coded `'THE WARDEN'` at L1329.
- **D-02** Changes to Yama's `DATA.BOSSES.yama` entry:
  - `name` stays `'YAMA DHARMARAJA'`.
  - The Devanagari `'यम धर्मराज'` (`UI.DEVANAGARI.bosses.yama`) stays **unchanged**.
  - New `title: 'THE TRIAL OF DHARMA'`, shown above the name in the boss intro.
  - `sub: 'Lord of Dharma — The Final Judgment'`
  - `intro: '"Abhimanyu. Sixteen rings you have broken to stand here. No soul leaves my realm untested. Show me that your dharma holds when death itself weighs it."'` This is the "Trial of Dharma" draft from 04-CONTEXT `<flagged_for_user>`.
  - `lore` is unchanged. It is already reverent.
- **D-03** The phase-2 banner stays `'THE LEDGER IS OPENED'`. Move names stay `ledger` / `danda` ("Danda of Judgment"), per 04-CONTEXT D-13. They are already trial vocabulary.
- **D-04** When the Trial is completed, Yama's HP reaching 0 is presented as the **Trial being passed**, not as a death.
  - `ROOMS.onBossDefeated()` shows the banner `'THE TRIAL IS PASSED'` on the last biome, before `GAME.onVictory()`.
  - `killEnemy()` handles Yama this way:
    - It does not count Yama in `GAME.kills` (the "Foes slain" stat). Yama is not a foe.
    - It uses the existing gold `'phase'` VFX preset instead of `'bossDeath'`.
    - It skips the `'bossdie'` sfx. `onBossDefeated` already plays `'punya'`.
  - The `e.dead = true` state and every game-logic consequence are **unchanged**. This is a mechanics-neutral presentation change.
- **D-05** Dying during the Trial (`GAME.bossRef.type === 'yama'` and Yama not yet `.dead`) changes the death screen:
  - The header reads `'SENT BACK'` instead of `'SLAIN'`.
  - The line comes from the new `DATA.TRIAL_DEATH_LINES`, in which Abhimanyu is weighed and sent back.
  - Every other death keeps `'SLAIN'` and `DATA.DEATH_LINES`.
- **D-06** Victory copy: keep the two existing `DATA.VICTORY_LINES`, which already lean into the trial. Add one explicit Trial line.
- **D-07** God dialogue: audit **all** of `DIALOGUE.LINES`, not just `*_lastdeath_yama`. Any line about Yama says he judged, weighed or sent Abhimanyu back. The known offender is `indra_lastdeath_yama` ("Yama bested you"). The plan gives a rewrite. `dur_lastdeath_yama` and `dur_naraka` already comply.
- **D-08** Player-facing docs: README L44 "final boss: **Yama Dharmaraja**" becomes "final trial". `index.html`'s meta description ("fight your way out of Yama's realm") is acceptable as is, because the player fights the realm's asuras, not Yama.
- **D-09** Reverence lint (smoke). It reuses the existing 05.5 lint pattern at test/smoke.js ~L2749 and extends it to all player-facing strings: a deep walk of `DATA`, `DIALOGUE.LINES`/`LORE`, `PHASE2_TITLES`, and the visible text of `index.html`. It has three rules:
  - (a) **No deity is the object of a hostile verb.** The forms caught are verb + deity, and deity + "is/was" + slain/killed/defeated.
  - (b) **No sentence that names Yama or Dharmaraja contains hostile or enemy vocabulary.**
  - (c) **Yama's label, title, sub and intro use trial vocabulary.**
- **D-10** Vocabulary standard for all future strings, including Phase 6 achievement names. **"Complete the Trial of Dharma"** and **"Pass Yama's judgment"** are allowed. "Defeat/kill/slay/beat Yama" is never allowed. The D-09 lint enforces this for anything stored in `DATA`.

### Art (Plan 02)
- **D-11 Pipeline.** FLUX.1-schnell Q4_K_S GGUF via ComfyUI-GGUF, as shipped in 05.5-04:
  - 4 steps, cfg 1, euler/simple
  - positive-only natural-language prompts, at most 180 words (the gen_subject LINT)
  - no negated nouns
  - a composition anchor as the first clause
  - held props pressed close to the body
- **D-12 Bosses on Flux.** `gen_subject.py` gains non-god Flux routing:
  - `_profile_name_for` honours a per-entry `checkpoint_profile` for any kind.
  - Kinds `boss`/`enemy` on a `flux_gguf` profile use `entry.flux_subject` + the new top-level `boss_style_positive_flux`, at `boss_size_flux` `[896, 1152]`.
  - Seeds come from `flux_base_seed + attempt`.
  - Every other legacy subject's `--dry-run` output must stay **byte-identical**.
- **D-13 Boss finishing.** Finishing goes through `rebuild_atlases.py` (the 05.6-03 recipe):
  - birefnet-general matte
  - despeckle
  - LANCZOS fit to 512 (margin 4+outline)
  - UnsharpMask r1.2/p60/t2
  - baked `#140b08` outline: yama 5px, kumbhakarna 4px
  - `phase2_variant --scale 2`: yama `divine`, kumbhakarna `ember`
  - pack with 8px padding
  - animation keys `idle` + `phase2` unchanged

  A new additive `--src KEY=PATH` override lets a regenerated raw replace the recovered source. Under it, `recovered.json`/`legacy_check.json` are optional, because both are gitignored and absent in this worktree.
- **D-14 Portraits.** Agni and Indra re-run the **shipped recipe**: the same `flux_subject`, plus a minimal targeted prompt fix, starting at the **shipped seed**. The goal is to keep the approved composition. Finishing uses `god_finish` (birefnet-general, no keyfill) via `finish_portrait.py`.
  - Agni starts at attempt 0 (seed 71150, shipped). Next are 71151..71155.
  - Indra starts at attempt 1 (seed 71251, shipped). Next are 71252..71256. 71250 was rejected in 05.5-04 as a trident.
- **D-15 Env on the env pipeline — Flux FIRST (orchestrator override 2026-10-19, per user decision UD-2 "Fix now with Flux").** Naraka `gate` and `vent_grate` stay on the env pipeline (`gen_batch.py` → `finish_env` via `env_build.py --only`), but generation uses `--ckpt-profile flux1_schnell_q4ks` first, with a per-entry `flux_subject` + `env_style_positive_flux` through the same D-12 routing.
  - Style-consistency gate: each Flux candidate is reviewed at game scale next to its Naraka neighbours (floor tile, wallface, columns, props) in a review grid. If it reads as a different art style (lighting, rendering, palette) and the env_grade pass in finish_env cannot reconcile it, fall back to the kit's `sdxl_base` cutout profile (up to 3 attempts) and record why.
  - New seed ranges: gate `61500+`, vent_grate `61510+`.
  - The old accepted seeds are recorded as `superseded`.
- **D-16 Hole-punch fix.** The ad-hoc BFS flood-fill from 05.4-02 becomes `finish_env.hole_punch()`:
  - It is seeded at the given points (default: the image centre).
  - It uses a colour-distance tolerance against the seed pixel.
  - It only commits if the filled region never touches the image border.

  `env_build.py` applies it when the subject entry has `"hole_punch": true` (or `[[x,y],...]` seeds). The new torana gate (an enclosed doorway opening) uses it. The vent uses it only if rembg filled a lattice gap with backdrop grey.
- **D-17 `env_build.py --only` must preserve the manifest.** Today `merge_manifest` keeps only the keys that were rebuilt. Running `--only gate,vent_grate` would **drop every other Naraka tile and frame**. Fix: carry over unchanged existing tile/frame entries for keys not rebuilt. The Naraka `_gen/env` raws for the other 15 subjects are absent here, so a full rebuild is impossible and `--only` is the only path.
- **D-18 Attempts.** Up to 6 attempts per asset. Rewrite the prompt between rounds rather than reseeding blindly (the 02-11/05.5-04 lesson). Record every rejected seed and its reason. An asset that fails 6 times is recorded **NOT SHIPPED**, and the old asset stays with its cultural flag.
- **D-19 GPU gate.** Plan 02 Task 1 (tooling, no GPU) may run any time. Generation waits until **05.4-03 is complete AND the orchestrator explicitly opens the GPU gate**. Requirements before generating:
  - the ComfyUI `/queue` is idle
  - no `gen_subject.py`/`gen_batch.py` process is running
  - Plan 02 is not run concurrently with 05.4-05, because both touch `naraka-env.json`, `PROVENANCE.json` and `AI-DISCLOSURE.md`

  All Flux jobs run first and SDXL env jobs run afterwards. Never run them concurrently, because that thrashes models on 16 GB.
- **D-20 Cultural status.** Every regenerated asset's provenance gets `cultural_review: "pending"` (QA-03 human sign-off is still Phase 7). The self-review text lists the iconography checks.

## Iconography Specs (hard checks for Plan 02 self-review)

All assets must also pass the 05.5 hard checks where applicable:
- H2: no menace on gods
- H3: no sexualization
- H4: no gore
- H6: no text or pseudo-script, and no swastika
- HX1: consistent skin colour
- HX3: anatomy and arm count
- HX4: the held prop intact in the finished cutout
- HX5: no halo, frame or border in the cutout

### Yama (boss sprite, idle + `divine` phase 2), CULT-02

| Requirement | Spec |
|---|---|
| Skin | Deep dark green or storm-cloud dark green-grey, the same on face, neck, arms and hands (HX1). **Never blue or blue-grey.** |
| Headwear | A low dark-bronze or copper crown band (mukuta) with a small buffalo-head emblem. **No tall gold kirita and no peacock feather.** |
| Attributes | A **danda**: a tall dark iron staff or rod held upright in one hand, topped with a **carved buffalo head**. A **pasha**: a coiled rope noose held in the other hand. Exactly two arms. |
| Buffalo motif | At least one clearly readable instance at 512 px: the danda finial, the crown emblem, or a belt clasp. **No horns on Yama's own head** (that reads as a demon or a Tibetan wrathful form). |
| Garments | Crimson or dark-red dhoti and sash, the traditional Yama red. Dark bronze ornaments. A black buffalo hide over one shoulder is optional. |
| Expression | Stern, calm, dignified and judge-like. Moustache allowed. **No fangs, snarl, red eyes or skulls.** |
| Must NOT read as | Vishnu or Krishna (blue skin, gold crown, chakra/disc, conch, peacock feather, four arms). Shiva (trident, third eye, crescent moon, snake). A demon. |
| Style | Matches the existing boss atlases (Kumbhakarna/Tataka): bold ink outline, painterly, chunky readable silhouette, full body head to feet. |

### Kumbhakarna (boss sprite, idle + `ember` phase 2), CULT-03

| Requirement | Spec |
|---|---|
| Keep | The established look in `assets/sprites/_raw/bosses/kumbhakarna/kumbhakarna_idle_0.png`: colossal golden-yellow/ochre-skinned rakshasa, broad brutish face with tusks, wild dark-gold mane, ornate bronze shoulder armour, huge round belly, bronze belt plate, loincloth, crouched heavy stance. The weapon is held upright at his side. |
| Change | The weapon is a **massive club or mace** (mudgara): a thick dark iron-banded wooden club with a heavy blunt head, optionally iron-studded. |
| Must NOT read as | A trident, trishula, spear, crescent-bladed staff or any pronged weapon (Shiva). Hanuman's golden ball-headed gada (avoid a round gold sphere-on-a-stick). |
| Also check | No vertical third-eye mark on the forehead (a Shaiva mark). The existing art has a forehead jewel, so check it explicitly. |

### Agni portrait, CULT-03
- **Remove only the forehead tilak.** The forehead must be bare, the same red-gold skin, with **no U/V-shaped urdhva-pundra, no vertical lines and no tripundra**.
- Everything else as shipped:
  - flame crown/hair
  - single calm bearded face
  - two arms
  - flame cupped in the raised right palm
  - sacred thread
  - red dhoti, gold jewellery

### Indra portrait, CULT-03
- Classic **double-ended vajra**: a short golden ritual thunderbolt held horizontally across the chest at its centre grip.
  - Each end has slender curved prongs (3-5) that bow outward and **close together into a single point**, like a closed lotus bud.
  - The two ends are mirror images.
  - The whole object lies on one straight axis.
- **Must NOT read as** a cross or four-pointed star (prongs splayed perpendicular), a trident, a Greek zigzag bolt or a hammer.
- Everything else as shipped:
  - kirita crown
  - moustache, fair golden skin
  - indigo/white/gold garments
  - two arms

### Naraka gate (env frame, role `gate`, worldH 210), CULT-04
- An **Indian torana / temple gateway** in the Naraka kit palette (deep maroon, charcoal and ember, muted):
  - two thick carved square pillars of dark basalt and blackened iron
  - a heavy stepped lintel with a curved, scroll-ended crossbeam (Sanchi-torana-like) or a temple-doorway frame
  - an open doorway glowing with ember light
  - optional iron chains
- Carving is **lotus/vine scrollwork and geometric bands only**: no human, animal-deity or idol figures (env cultural check, 05.4 D-09).
- **Must NOT read as** Gothic or European: no pointed arch, portcullis, cathedral tracery or castle crenellation.
- The enclosed doorway opening must be transparent after finishing (D-16 hole-punch).

### Naraka vent_grate (env hazard-skin for `fireVent`, round, ground_safe), CULT-04
- A **round carved stone or iron floor vent**:
  - a thick carved basalt ring rim with a plain geometric notched band
  - covered by a heavy lattice of crossed square iron bars or a simple square-hole stone jali
  - ember glow beneath
  - soot at the rim
  - top-down
- **Must NOT read as** a hubcap or wheel: no radial spokes and no central hub boss.
- **No lotus, mandala, yantra or any sacred symbol.** It is a walked-on ground surface, and walking on sacred symbols is disrespectful (05.4 ground_safe rule).
