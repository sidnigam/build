# Phase 05.7.1 Plan 07: Boss AI Variety Summary

**Bosses and mini-bosses now pick moves with weighted random selection and never use the same move twice in a row. Picks are shaped by the player's distance (close/mid/far bands), and area moves are favoured against a player who stands still or hugs a wall. Attacks come in 2-3 move combo chains with 0.2-0.38 s links. Each biome boss also gains two new telegraphed moves on the plan-02 projectile families and statuses. Yama's two are framed as judgments: The Pasha Binds and The Scales Are Weighed.**

## Accomplishments

### Move selection (Task 1, D-21, COMBAT-17)
- **`BOSS_AI` constants:**
  - Bands: close < 220 px, mid < 480 px, far beyond that.
  - In-band moves get ×2 weight, out-of-band ×0.3, and `'any'` moves are unscaled.
  - Camping means 3 s within 60 px of one point, or being within 90 px of a wall. It doubles the weight of `area` moves.
  - Chain lengths are rolled per phase: phase 1 picks 1/2/3 moves with weights .25/.55/.2; phase 2 picks 2/3 with weights .55/.45.
  - `CHAIN_REST`: links rest 0.28-0.38 s in phase 1 and 0.20-0.28 s in phase 2.
- **Tags:** all 17 boss move defs and all 13 mini move defs carry `band` + `area`.
- **`pickMove()` rewrite:**
  - A pending phase-2 opener always goes first.
  - `cands = table.filter(m => m[0] !== this.lastMove)`, so a move can never repeat back-to-back (strict no-repeat).
  - Candidates are weighted by band and camp state, then chosen with `U.weightedChoice`.
  - Picking `chase` breaks the chain.
- **Camp tracker:** `trackCamp(dt)` takes a sample every 0.25 s and keeps the last 12 (3 s). It runs in `Boss.update` after the stun early return. `camping()` reads that history plus the wall rule.
- **Chain rest:** after the state switch, any move that just entered `rest` while `chainLeft > 0` has its rest cut to `U.rand(...CHAIN_REST[phase]) * (GAME.heat?.restMul || 1)`. Moves that link straight into `pickMove` (summon, teleport, spiral) stay immediate links.
- **Mini-bosses** get all of this through the plan-06 hooks without any changes to their code.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Chase mid-chain made chain lengths unreliable**
- **Found during:** Task 1 design.
- **Issue:** The planned `pickMove` resets `chainLeft` to 0 whenever `chase` is picked. If chase came up mid-chain, the chain was cut to 1 move, which breaks "phase-2 chain lengths are 2 or 3".
- **Fix:** Mid-chain picks drop `chase` from the candidates whenever any other candidate exists. Chase can still start a chain, which cancels it.
- **Files modified:** js/entities.js
- **Commit:** cbe6b0c

**2. [Rule 1 - Test correctness] Band and camp share metrics are conditional on the previous move**
- **Found during:** Task 1 GREEN.
- **Issue:** Strict no-repeat caps any single move at 50% of picks. A naive "share ≥ 1.5 × base share" therefore cannot be met by tables dominated by one move. Examples: tataka p1 has radial as its only area move; ahiravana p1 at far range is mostly teleport.
- **Fix:** Shares are now measured over picks whose previous move was outside the target set, against the base-weight share over the same candidate sets. The odds ratio is used once the base share is above 0.4, where a 1.5× share ratio is mathematically capped.
- **Other test changes:** each `room()` reseeds `U`, so the checks are deterministic. Pick counts were raised to 3000-6000.
- **Files modified:** test/combat/bossai.test.js
- **Commit:** cbe6b0c
