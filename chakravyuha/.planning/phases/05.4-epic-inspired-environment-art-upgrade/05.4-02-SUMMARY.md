## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] rembg left enclosed openings (gate archway, fire_pit ring center) filled with opaque backdrop grey instead of transparent**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (Naraka finished-pass review)
- **Issue:** `naraka_gate_0.png` and `naraka_fire_pit_0.png`'s finished frames had their enclosed interior regions (the arch doorway; the ring's hollow middle) at alpha=253 with RGB matching the raw generation's backdrop grey almost exactly -- a known rembg/u2net limitation where enclosed "holes" inside a salient blob aren't detected as background.
- **Fix:** Wrote a one-off BFS flood-fill script (seeded at the image center, bounded by a color-distance tolerance against the seed pixel, only committing the fix if the filled region never touches the image border) and applied it directly to both finished frame PNGs, then repacked the atlas. Not added to `finish_env.py`'s shared pipeline, to avoid false-positive holes on props with legitimately opaque enclosed regions (e.g. `iron_cauldron`'s interior, which was checked and left untouched).
- **Files modified:** `assets/sprites/_raw/env/naraka/frames/naraka_gate_0.png`, `assets/sprites/_raw/env/naraka/frames/naraka_fire_pit_0.png`, `assets/sprites/env/naraka/naraka-env-atlas.png` (repack)
- **Verification:** Visual review on checker background confirmed clean transparent openings after the fix; `node tools/check-provenance.js` and `node test/smoke.js` stayed green
- **Committed in:** `c5ba817` (Task 1 commit)

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Corrected the checkpoint-per-kind mechanism from a global `env_checkpoint` toggle to a per-call `--ckpt-profile` override**
- **Found during:** mid-session, flagged by the orchestrator after a rate-limit interruption
- **Issue:** An earlier implementation toggled the shared `prompts.json env_checkpoint` value to `dreamshaper_xl_alpha2` for the duration of the floor/wallface batch and reverted it afterward. While reverted correctly in this run (verified: no cutout-kind subject was generated while the toggle was active), this approach leaves ambient global state that a crashed/interrupted process could strand mid-toggle, affecting unrelated concurrent work.
- **Fix:** Switched to `gen_batch.py --ckpt-profile dreamshaper_xl_alpha2` on explicit subject-key lists for every subsequent DreamShaper generation (the 2 wallface retries), with accepted DreamShaper raw files copied (not moved) from their `--ckpt-profile`-suffixed filename to the unsuffixed filename `env_build.py._raw_paths` expects. `env_checkpoint` itself was set back to and left at `sdxl_base` in the committed config.
- **Files modified:** `tools/art/prompts.json` (`env_checkpoint_policy`, `env_checkpoint` value)
- **Verification:** `env_build.py {naraka,patala} --check` found no missing raw files for any DreamShaper-profile subject; both biomes built and packed successfully
- **Committed in:** `c5ba817`, `f5bbc67`

## Issues Encountered
- **ComfyUI ran slowly under memory pressure for stretches of the Patala batch** (observed `ram_free` as low as ~1.3GB of 16GB total): several generations that normally take ~3-5 minutes took 10-18 minutes (e.g. `patala:bronze_lamp` attempt 1 took 1088.6s, `patala:column` took 719.6s). One retry (`patala:bronze_lamp` attempt 2) hit the client-side 900s wait timeout while ComfyUI was still processing; resumed cleanly with `gen_subject.py --wait-only <prompt_id>` rather than re-queueing a duplicate job. No generation was lost to this.
- **A background generation monitor appeared to stall across a session interruption** (flagged by the orchestrator's resume message). On investigation, the underlying `gen_batch.py` process had in fact completed successfully days/hours earlier in wall-clock terms within the session; only the polling loop watching it had been interrupted. No work was lost or needed to be redone -- confirmed via `git status`, `ps`, ComfyUI `/queue`, and the gen_batch log files before resuming.
- **Composition failures requiring prompt rewrites** (not just reseeding), consistent with the 02-08/02-11 "rewrite after repeated failure" lesson: `naraka:wallface` (perspective vista), `naraka:gate` (rembg fg/bg inversion), `naraka:scroll_pile` (scattered clusters), `patala:wallface` (isolated-render composition, then a crop-tightening pass), `patala:gem_pile` (edge-to-edge tiling), `patala:fallen_pillar` (scattered fragments), `patala:bronze_lamp` (3 distinct failure modes, ultimately dropped). All documented per-subject above with seeds and reasons.
- **A flaky (non-reproducing) smoke-test assertion** ("god presence on boon panel", 05.5 territory) was observed intermittently and logged to `deferred-items.md` rather than investigated, per the SCOPE BOUNDARY rule -- it is outside this plan's file ownership and was not reliably reproducible (5+ consecutive clean reruns both before and after this plan's commits).
