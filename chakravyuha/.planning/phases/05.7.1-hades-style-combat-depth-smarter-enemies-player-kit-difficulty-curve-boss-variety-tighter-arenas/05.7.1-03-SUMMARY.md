# Phase 05.7.1 Plan 03: Hades-Style Player Kit and Lodged-Astra Cast Summary

**This plan adds weapon-specific dash-strikes (Khanda lunge, Gandiva volley, Gada leap), dash-cancels, hold-to-charge heavies (thrust, smash, Gandiva perfect release), distinct finishers and specials that weave into the combo string. Base tempo is faster: speed 305, dash recharge 1.5 s, quicker swings and shorter special cooldowns. Cast astras now lodge where they land and are walked over to recover them, with safety returns. All six cast boons still work.**

## UX change for the plan-11 playtest

**Holding attack no longer auto-swings.** Taps still flow through the full combo string, and a 0.15 s buffer catches presses made during recovery. Holding the button for 0.28 s after a swing starts charging the heavy variant, which fires on release (melee needs at least 0.35 charge). If the user dislikes this, the fallback in RESEARCH Open Question 1 is to charge on special-hold instead.

## Accomplishments

- **Tempo (D-02, COMBAT-01 player side):**
  - `DATA.KIT.speed 305` (was 265) and `dashCd 1.5` (was 2.2).
  - Weapon rates are 0.23 / 0.16 / 0.47 for khanda / gandiva / gada (were 0.27 / 0.18 / 0.55).
  - Special cooldowns are 3 / 3.5 / 4.5 (were 4 / 5 / 6).
- **Dash-strike (D-12):** an attack within 0.2 s after a dash ends, or during the dash, triggers the weapon's dash-strike. It deals `src: 'attack'` damage, so Agni burn, Krishna maya, Indra chain, Hanuman damage and Pavanaputra all apply. The test checks the Agni burn.
  - Khanda: lunges up to 90 px, then a 1.4 rad cone at ×1.5.
  - Gandiva: 3 arrows at ±0.18 rad, each `lerp(0.6) × 1.2`, life 0.45 s.
  - Gada: 0.18 s hop of up to 110 px, then a 120 px slam at ×1.6 with a 0.3 s stun.
- **Dash-cancel (D-13):** a dash zeroes `atkCd` and any charge or buffered press. `comboIdx` and `comboT` survive.
- **Charged variants (D-13):**
  - Khanda: piercing 220 × 34 px thrust at `combo[2] × 2.2 × max(0.5, charge)`.
  - Gada: 170 px smash at `combo[0] × 2.5`, 0.6 s stun, camera punch and chargeFlash.
  - Gandiva: releasing within 0.12 s of full draw gives a perfect shot: ×1.5, pierce, knockback 300, a gold arrow and chargeFlash. The screen flash plays only when the screenFlash setting is on.
- **Finishers:**
  - Khanda's 3rd hit is a 2.6 rad cross-cut with a ring.
  - Gada's 2nd hit adds a shockwave out to range + 60 at 0.5×. Each enemy is hit at most once.
  - Gandiva's third quick shot within 0.9 s becomes a 3-arrow fan.
- **Specials:** they no longer reset the combo, and `comboT` stays at 0.6 or more.
- **Lodged astra (D-14):**
  - Timed regen is removed. A cast that ends (range, wall or non-pierce hit) lodges at its end point, pushed inside the arena and out of obstacles.
  - There is a 90 px magnet and pickup at `P.r + 18`.
  - Safety returns: after 8 s, on room clear, on boss defeat, on room change (which also returns in-flight casts) and on a defiance revive.
  - Cast boons: kri_cast lodges at the player when the orbit expires, and vayu_cast lodges where the cyclone dies. agni_cast also drops a pool where a missed astra lodges.
  - The ammo invariant `castCharges + lodged + in-flight <= castMax` holds every frame of a 60 s sim that cycles orbit, cyclone and projectile casts (15 casts, 0 violations).
- **Hit feel (COMBAT-12):** only the existing hooks are used: VFX presets, `VFX.hitstop` (dash-strike 2f, finisher 3f), `CAMERA.punch`, `CAMERA.kick` and `VFX.flash`. Four new presets go through `this.count()`, so reducedParticles scales them down. Pad rumble mappings: dashStrike → hit, chargeFlash → crit, finisherRing → crit.
- **Reads:**

  - HUD pips show lodged ammo as a bright outline with a centre dot.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Room change would lose in-flight cast ammo**
- **Found during:** Task 3 (designing the invariant)
- **Issue:** The plan's `enterRoom` returns only `G.lodged.length`. A cast still in flight (or an active orbit or cyclone) when the player walks through a door gets wiped with `pprojectiles/orbits/zones`, and that charge is gone for the rest of the run.
- **Fix:** `GAME.castAmmoOut()` counts lodged plus in-flight ammo. `enterRoom` returns that count, capped at `castMax`.
- **Files modified:** js/game.js, js/rooms.js
- **Commit:** ffcd2f6

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Astras that land after the room clears**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** The clear return runs once, at the moment of the clear. A cast already flying would then lodge in a cleared room and sit there for 8 s.
- **Fix:** The lodged loop returns everything whenever `state === 'cleared'`. `startRun` also resets `lodged`.
- **Commit:** ffcd2f6

**3. [Rule 2 - Correctness] Lodge points pushed out of obstacles**
- **Issue:** A cast stopped by a wall ends inside or against the obstacle, where the player might not be able to reach it.
- **Fix:** `lodgeAt` runs `clampToArena` on a 20 px probe, which gives the arena inset and pushes the point out of pillars, walls and pits.
- **Commit:** ffcd2f6

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug] dashStrikeUsed reset point**
- **Issue:** The plan resets `dashStrikeUsed` when the dash ends. That makes "`dashT > 0 ||` sinceDash" impossible during the dash itself, because the flag is still set from the last strike.
- **Fix:** The flag resets in `tryDash`, so you get one dash-strike per dash, either during it or within 0.2 s after.
- **Commit:** 87cde6c

**5. [Test fix] Test harness details**
- `PAD.bindings` is lazily loaded (null headless until the first poll), so the parity test calls `PAD.loadBindings()` before taking its snapshot.
- The orbit hit cooldown reads `GAME.time`, which only `GAME.frame()` advances. The cast suite's tick advances `GAME.time` the same way the balance sim does.
- The kri_cast boon dummy stands on the 70 px orbit path.
- **Commits:** 9383425, ffcd2f6
