# Phase 05.7.1 Plan 04: Smarter, Fairer Enemies (ENEMY_AI + ENEMY_TG) Summary

**A per-frame enemy coordinator in js/ai.js: slot rings with escape-route cutting, shared attack tokens, range bands and separation. All 8 existing enemy types now use it, and every damaging attack goes through a logged wind-up that meets its ENEMY_TG minimum. Archers fire needle shots and nagas spit poisoning venom. Enemies also engage faster after spawning.**

## Accomplishments

- **Attack tokens (D-06):** each category has a pool: `melee 2, ranged 2, heavy 1, support 1, bite 4`. Melee and ranged get +0/+1/+1/+2 per biome, and a `GAME.heat.tokens` hook (inert until plan 09) adds to every pool. In a 20 s sim, 10 rakshasas around a stationary player never have more than 2 winding up or lunging at the same time.
- **Slots and escape cut:** the melee ring has 8 slots at `70 + r` and the ranged ring has 10 slots at mid-band. Each enemy re-picks its slot every 0.5-1.0 s, not every frame. When the player moves, melee slot angles bend toward the heading by up to 0.7 rad, and slot choice favours slots ahead of the player. Test result: with the player moving at 300 px/s, mean cos(slotAngle) is 0.37. Slots outside the arena are penalised.
- **Range bands and the view band (D-18):** archers and nagas hold 260-380 px; the yatudhana holds 260-560 px. Ranged units only start a shot from inside `viewBand(P)` (half-extent `VW/(2Z) - 40` × `VH/(2Z) - 40`, Z = 1.3). Every recorded archer/naga shot in all sims was fired from inside the band. In the band test, archers spent 100% of frames inside [220, 440] px.
- **Telegraph invariant (D-11):** these minimums are enforced: melee 0.30 s, ranged 0.30 s, status 0.40 s, stun 0.50 s, beam 0.70 s, plus a 0.20 s contact bite. Timing variance runs ×1.0-1.3, so it only lengthens tells. 60 s room sims in all four biomes, each with its full roster, logged 107-234 armed attacks per biome with **0 violations** and 0 short wind-ups.
- **Per-enemy tells:**

  | Enemy | Tell |
  |-------|------|
  | Rakshasa | 0.45 s line, then lunge |
  | Archer | 0.35 s aim line that tracks the player, then needle |
  | Naga | 0.45 s cone, then 3 venom shots at ±0.3 rad |
  | Vetala | 0.5 s circle; it now blinks ahead of a running player |
  | Daitya | 0.7 s line, then charge |
  | Preta / Bhuta | 0.2 s bite circle (r + 8) |

  A stun freezes a wind-up mid-tell, and death releases the token.
- **Faster engage (D-02):** spawn grace drops from 0.6 s to 0.45 s, and the first attack timer from 0.3-1.2 s to 0.2-0.8 s. Both spawn-mandala renderers and the sprite fade use `DATA.WAVES.spawnGrace`.

## API as shipped (plan 05 builds on this)

### ENEMY_TG (js/ai.js)
- `MIN = { melee: .30, ranged: .30, status: .40, stun: .50, beam: .70, bite: .20 }`.
- `violations` holds strings `type:unarmedHit`, `type:unarmedFire` and `type:short:kind`. `windLog` holds `{type, kind, wind}`. `fireLog` holds `{type, inView}` and is capped at 500 entries.
- `log(list, entry)` is bounded by `LOG_MAX` (4000). `clear()` empties all three lists.

### ENEMY_AI (js/ai.js)
- **`CFG`:** `MELEE_R 70`, `RANGED_BAND [260, 380]`, `SUPPORT_BAND [260, 560]`, `SLOTS_MELEE 8`, `SLOTS_RANGED 10`, `REPICK [0.5, 1.0]`, `CUT_BIAS 0.7`, `CUT_SCORE 0.8`, `TOKENS {...}`, `TOKEN_BIOME [0, 1, 1, 2]`, `VIEW_ZOOM 1.3`, `VIEW_MARGIN 40`, `LONG_TELL_BONUS 0.3` (for plan 05 long shots), `SLOT_EDGE 12`, `CLOSE_IN 30`, `CONTACT_AI ['chase', 'tank']`, `SEP_PUSH 220`, `VAR [1.0, 1.3]`, `PV_RATE 8`.
- **State:** `holders` (category → Set), `slots` (Map enemy → `{ring, i}`), `pv` (smoothed player velocity).
- **Lifecycle:** `reset()` is called by `ROOMS.enterRoom`. `tick(dt)` is called by `GAME.update` right before the enemy loop. It updates `pv`, reclaims tokens, re-picks slots and runs separation.
- **Tokens:** `pool(cat)`, `requestToken(e, cat) -> bool` (sets `e.tokenCat`), `releaseToken(e)`, `holding(cat)`.
- **Slots:** `role(e)`, `ringOf(e)`, `slotAngle(ring, i)`, `slotPoint(e)`, `heading()`, `cutK()`.
- **Movement:** `steer(e, dt, mul = 1)`. It moves the enemy but does not clamp; Enemy.update clamps.
- **View band:** `viewBand(P) -> {x, y, w, h}` and `inViewBand(x, y, P)`. Both are pure.
- **Timing:** `windTime(kind, base)` returns `max(MIN[kind], base) × U.rand(1.0, 1.3)`.

### Enemy (js/entities.js)
- **New fields:** `windKind`, `windT`, `windTotal`, `armedAtk`, `slotT`, `tokenCat`, `biteDmg`, `aimA`.
- **`hurtPlayer(amount, opts)`:** returns `P.takeDamage(amount, this.x, this.y, opts)`. The source point gives knock direction and camera kick.
- **`fire(fam, vx, vy, dmg, extra)`:** returns the projectile, with `owner: this` merged into `extra`.
- **Wind-up lifecycle:**
  - `windUp(kind, secs, telegraph)` sets `this.t = secs`. `windT` accrues in `update()` after the stun early return.
  - `armAttack()` logs the wind-up, checks it against `MIN` and clears the telegraph.
  - `endAttack()` disarms and releases the token.
- **Contact damage:** `touchAttack(dToP, dt, dmg)` hits at once when the enemy is armed (lunge/charge). Otherwise it starts a bite if it gets a bite token, and `biteStep(P)` resolves the bite.
- **Damage hooks for plan 05:** new enemies should use `role` + `fam` in DATA and the same request → windUp → arm → resolve → endAttack lifecycle. Static scan rule: no `takeDamage(` or `enemyShot(` inside `class Enemy` outside `hurtPlayer`/`fire`.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Contact units could never reach biting range**
- **Found during:** Task 1 design of `steer`
- **Issue:** The plan's melee rule says to move to the slot point and close in once within 30 px of it. An enemy that closes in leaves its slot, gets pulled back to it, and ends up hovering about `40 + r` px from the player. That is outside contact reach (`r + P.r + 4`), so pretas and bhutas could never bite.
- **Fix:** Contact AIs (chase/tank) close in on the player whenever they are within `MELEE_R + r + 30` of it. Other melee units hold their slot until they win a token.
- **Files modified:** js/ai.js
- **Commit:** 75e4564

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Mid-band ranged slots would block vertical shooters**
- **Issue:** The view band's half-height is only about 237 px. A slot at 320 px above or below the player is outside it, so an archer sitting in that slot could never fire.
- **Fix:** The ranged slot radius is `min(mid, edgeDist(angle) - 12)`, and steer's "too close" threshold drops to match. Every ranged slot is now a legal firing position.
- **Commit:** 75e4564

**3. [Rule 2 - Correctness] Token leaks, view-band drift and spawn fade**
- `tick()` also reclaims tokens from enemies that are no longer attacking or no longer in `GAME.enemies`. Smoke tests and room code replace `GAME.enemies` without killing anyone.
- A shot whose shooter left the view band during the tell is aborted rather than fired. This keeps the D-18 rule true at release time, not only at wind-up start.
- `SPRITES.CFG.SPAWN_DUR` is now 0.45. Otherwise the sprite-path fade would start at 25% alpha with the shorter grace.
- **Commits:** 75e4564, 98f1635

**4. [Design detail] Shot colour**
- `Enemy.fire` passes no colour for non-bolt families, so PROJ uses the family's data colour. The plan's literal call passed `this.color`, which would have tinted needles the archer's gold and weakened the "distinct families" read.
- **Commit:** 98f1635

**5. [Test detail] Extra checks beyond the plan's behaviour list**
- Extra checks:
  - flankers at ±80-100° pick slots ahead of the player
  - a stationary player gives no heading bias
  - slot hysteresis
  - archer aim tell
  - stun freezes a wind-up
  - killEnemy releases the token
  - SPAWN_DUR parity
- The biome sims spawn one of each roster type, kill one enemy every 3 s so waves keep cycling, and alternate player runs and stands. With a continuous stroll, the player simply outran the pretas.

**Total deviations:** 3 auto-fixed (2 bugs, 1 correctness group) plus a colour detail and test-only additions. No architectural changes.
