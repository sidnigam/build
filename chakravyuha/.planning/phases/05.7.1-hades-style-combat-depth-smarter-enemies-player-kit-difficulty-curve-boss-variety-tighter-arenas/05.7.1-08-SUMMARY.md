# Phase 05.7.1 Plan 08: Hades-Close Context Camera Zoom with Threat Indicators Summary

**The camera now sits at 1.3x in combat (a 984.6 x 553.8 world-px view). It eases out to 1.15x in boss and mini-boss rooms and in to 1.4x in rest rooms. Room changes snap to the new zoom, and the hub stays at identity. Kill-zoom punches pulse on top of the base zoom. Off-view threats and lodged astras always get an indicator, and the camera leans up to 60 px toward winding threats.**

## Accomplishments

- **Zoom math (D-18):** `xform()` is now `tx = Z·(F.x − S.x) − z·F.x + k.x` with `tz = z = Z·zoomPulse()`. At Z = 1 this matches the old 05.1 formula exactly; the camera suite checks it against the old expression. Results at Z ∈ {1, 1.15, 1.3, 1.4}:
  - `worldToScreen(S) = (0, 0)`
  - round-trip error ≤ 1e-9
  - `viewRect = VW/Z × VH/Z`
  - the punch focus maps to `Z·(F − S) + k`
- **Scroll clamp at zoom:** `clampScroll(dx, dy, R, z = this.Z)` uses `hi = R.x + R.w − VW/z`. `desiredScroll` subtracts `anchor/z`, so the player lands exactly on the screen anchor at 1.3x. In combat, boss and rest rooms the zoomed view stays inside the world rect, so there are no blank margins and Z never drops below 1.
- **Context zoom (D-19):** `zoomTarget()` picks:
  - hub or no player: 1
  - boss or mini-boss (live `bossRef`/`miniRef`, or a boss-size arena): 1.15
  - sarovar or kubera: 1.4
  - combat: 1.3
  - oversized arenas (> 1.05 × combat area): 1.2. This does nothing until 05.7.3 adds per-room sizes.

  `snap()` sets `Z = ZT` immediately, so `ROOMS.enterRoom` goes combat 1.3 → rest 1.4 → boss 1.15 with no swoop. `follow()` eases Z at 3/s and is within 0.01 of the target after 2 s.
- **Pixi/bridge alignment:** with a 1.3 base zoom plus punch and kick, worldContainer, the bridge counter-transform and `worldToScreen` agree to within 1e-6.
- **Threat bias:** `threatBias(P, enemies)` points at the centroid of threats within 900 px. Its size is `min(60, 0.15·d)`. `follow()` eases it in at `THREAT_RATE 2`, which reaches about 2 px in the first frame and the full 60 px after a few seconds. It eases back out when the wind-up ends, and `snap()` clears it. `LOOK_AIM 0.06 / MAX 40 / MOVE 20` are unchanged (quick 261019-nnu).
- **Indicators:**
  - `threat` arrows are size-12 `#ff4430` triangles with a bold 16px `!` behind them. Alpha pulses as `0.6 + 0.4·sin(10t)`, and holds at 1 when `screenFlash` is off.
  - `astra` indicators are a gold `#ffd34d` ring (r 11) with `◎` and a small triangle.
  - Both kinds survive `IND_MAX` truncation, even with `max: 0`. Threat buckets sort ahead of boss and enemy buckets.
- **View band (COMBAT-04):** `ENEMY_AI.viewBand(P).w === 2·(VW/(2·ZOOM.combat) − VIEW_MARGIN)` (904.6 × 473.8), and it reads `CFG.ZOOM.combat` when called.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Boss rooms would have snapped to the combat zoom and then eased out**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** `ROOMS.enterRoom` calls `CAMERA.snap()` before it assigns `GAME.bossRef` and `GAME.miniRef`. A `zoomTarget()` keyed only on those refs would snap boss and mini rooms to 1.3, then swoop out to 1.15.
- **Fix:** `zoomTarget()` also treats `GAME.arenaKind === 'boss'` (set by `setArena` before the snap) as the boss context.
- **Files modified:** js/renderer.js
- **Commit:** 9ba92b6

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Run start would have swooped the zoom**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** On a real run start, `enterRoom`'s snap runs while `GAME.state` is still `'hub'`. That forces Z = 1, and the first combat frames would then ease from 1 to 1.3.
- **Fix:** `follow()` snaps Z to the target on the first frame after a no-target (hub / no player) frame. The camera suite checks this ("first combat frame after the hub snaps Z").
- **Files modified:** js/renderer.js
- **Commit:** 9ba92b6

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] shot_stats.py needs PIL**
- **Found during:** Task 2 readability re-check
- **Issue:** The system `python3` has no PIL.
- **Fix:** Ran the script with `~/ComfyUI/venv/bin/python`, the interpreter tools/art/README.md designates for all art scripts. This used the CPU only; no ComfyUI job ran.
- **Commit:** none (verification only)
