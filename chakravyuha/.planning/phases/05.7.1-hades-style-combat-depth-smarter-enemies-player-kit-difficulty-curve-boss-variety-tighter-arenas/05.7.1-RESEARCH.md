## User Constraints (from CONTEXT.md)

### Locked Decisions

#### Difficulty, pace & run length
- **D-01: Base difficulty is Hades-like.** A first-time player usually dies in biome 1-2, and a full clear takes several runs with meta upgrades. Enemies hit harder and more often than today; today is "too easy and predictable". Tune with the new headless balance sim (D-26), not by feel alone.
- **D-02: Faster all round.**
  - The player is about 15% faster, with snappier attack recovery.
  - Enemies engage faster.
  - Waves overlap: the next wave spawns before the previous one is fully cleared. This extends 05.1 `DATA.WAVES.nextWaveAt` and keeps the `maxAlive` cap.
  - A combat room clears in about 45-75 s.
- **D-03: A full successful run (4 biomes + Yama) takes about 25-35 min.** Room count per biome is adjusted toward fewer, denser rooms if needed to hit this.
- **D-04: Heat follows the Pact of Punishment model.**
  - It unlocks after the first full clear.
  - The player toggles individual modifiers, each worth N Heat. Example modifiers: enemy HP+, enemy damage+, extra elites, faster enemies, boss phase-3/extra moves, room timer, fewer heals or no Sacred Pool, more projectiles.
  - Total Heat raises rewards (Punya/meta currency), and best Heat cleared is recorded per weapon.
  - It is persisted through the existing STORE/META save path (05 save migration).
  - The exact modifier list and values are Claude's discretion (see below).

#### Enemies, mini-bosses & elites
- **D-05: Add 8 new regular enemy types, 2 per biome (16 total).**
  - Each biome gets a signature cast in addition to the shared types.
  - Every new type has a distinct combat role. Examples: shield-bearer that must be flanked or broken, bomber/mortar, sniper with a long-tell line shot, fast swarm, healer/buffer, trapper, burrower or blinker, reflector.
  - All new enemies are asuras, rakshasas, pretas, nagas or other non-deity beings. Gods are never enemies (CLAUDE.md).
- **D-06: Smarter AI for all enemies, new and the existing 8.**
  - Flanking and surrounding (slot-based positioning around the player).
  - Ranged units keep distance and reposition.
  - Attack timing varies inside readable telegraphs.
  - Enemies don't all attack on the same beat (attack tokens/coordination).
  - Melee units cut off escape routes.
- **D-07: Elite modifiers.** Elites carry 1-2 visible modifiers (aura and colour tint plus a name tag), e.g. Armored, Hasty, Vampiric, Splitting, Shielded, Explosive on death. Elite share keeps the per-biome `eliteShare` curve from 05.1 D-19 and is raised per D-01. The exact list is Claude's discretion.
- **D-08: 4 mini-bosses, one per biome, in a fixed mid-biome room** (about room 4 of the biome).
  - The door shows a distinct mini-boss symbol, and the reward is a guaranteed boon or a better reward.
  - Each mini-boss has its own moveset, telegraphs (≥0.4 s, Phase 4 D-07) and phase change or enrage.
  - Mini-bosses are asura/rakshasa figures from the Mahabharata/Ramayana. Their identities are Claude's discretion, but they must be culturally plausible and are flagged for the Phase 7 QA-03 cultural review.

#### Enemy projectiles & player status effects
- **D-09: About 8-10 full projectile families**, each with its own shape, colour, trail, hit VFX and sound:
  - needle volley (fast, small)
  - big slow orb
  - homing wisp
  - splitting bomb
  - beam/line (long tell)
  - ground-AoE mortar (target circle)
  - boomerang chakra
  - expanding wave/ring
  - plus status-specific variants

  `GAME.eprojectiles` (today one generic struct `{x,y,vx,vy,dmg,color,r,t}`) becomes data-driven projectile types, e.g. `DATA.PROJECTILES`.
- **D-10: Statuses enemies can inflict on the player:**
  - **Stun.** 0.3-0.6 s with a clear wind-up. Diminishing returns plus brief stun immunity afterwards, so the player can never be stun-locked.
  - **Slow/root.** Venom, webs or naga coils. Dash breaks a root.
  - **Burn/poison DoT.** Fire (Naraka) burns; venom (Patala) stacks poison.
  - **Knockback/pull.** Heavy hits shove the player into hazards or walls; some attacks pull the player in.

  Every status has a clear on-player read: icon or pip, tint, particles, and the HUD.
- **D-11: The fairness rules still hold.** Every damaging or status attack has a readable telegraph. Dash i-frames still avoid hits. Player-facing statuses respect existing accessibility toggles (flash, shake, reduced particles).

#### Player kit
- **D-12: Hades-style dash-strike.** Attacking within about 0.2 s after a dash triggers a weapon-specific dash attack with bonus damage:
  - Khanda: lunge-slash
  - Gandiva: point-blank volley
  - Gada: leaping slam

  Boons can modify dash-strikes.
- **D-13: Cancels and finisher variants.**
  - Dash cancels any attack recovery.
  - Each weapon gets a hold/charged variant: Khanda charged thrust, Gada wind-up smash, Gandiva power shot. Gandiva already has a charge mechanic, so extend it.
  - Each weapon gets a distinct, satisfying finisher.
  - No full branching combo trees.
- **D-14: Hades cast model.**
  - The cast/astra fires a lodged projectile that drops where it lands; the player walks over it to pick it back up.
  - Ammo is limited, e.g. 2.
  - Specials get shorter cooldowns and can be woven into combos.
  - Existing boons that affect cast/special must keep working, or be migrated with smoke coverage.
- **D-15: Hit feel improves** through existing Phase 3 hooks: hitstop weights, camera punch, flash, VFX presets. Tune per new attack; don't build a new system.
- **D-16: Controller parity.** Every new input (dash-strike, hold/charge, cast pickup) works on keyboard/mouse and gamepad (05/05.2 INPUT bindings and DualSense), including rebinding.

#### Art for new characters

  - Provenance is recorded for every asset, and `node tools/check-provenance.js` must stay green.
  - The machine has 16 GB of RAM and GPU work is slow under memory pressure. Restart ComfyUI before long batches, and serialize GPU work.

#### Camera
- **D-18: Base combat zoom is about 1.3x** (a view of about 985x555 world px instead of 1280x720), close to Hades framing.
  - The 05.1 off-screen indicators and aim look-ahead must cover threats outside the view.
  - Ranged enemies should not routinely fire from off-screen. The AI prefers on-screen or edge firing positions, or gives a stronger warning for long shots.
- **D-19: Context zoom.**
  - Combat uses the base zoom.
  - The camera eases out slightly for bosses and large arenas, and eases in for small rooms and quiet moments (rest/shop).
  - Kill-zoom punches (Phase 3 D-14) stay and compose on top.
  - The scroll clamp and bridge alignment (05.1 D-05..D-12) must be correct at every zoom.

- **D-22: +2 new moves per boss** (Tataka, Ahiravana, Kumbhakarna, Yama), using the new projectile families and statuses. Phase 1 starts with more variety.

## Project Constraints (from CLAUDE.md)

- **Vanilla JS stays.** No framework, no module system. New code uses globals (`const X = {...}`) loaded by `<script>` tags in `index.html`, and is added to the `files` list in `test/smoke.js` in the same order.
- **AI-generated art only.** No commissioned art. Every atlas needs a complete `assets/sprites/PROVENANCE.json` entry; `node tools/check-provenance.js` must stay green.
- **Platform:** Steam Windows and macOS (Electron 42). Any new `js/*.js` file is already covered by the `build.files` glob `js/**/*`.
- **`node test/smoke.js` must stay green after every phase.** It currently passes 972 checks in about 94 s (measured 2026-11-08).
- **Mythological reverence:** gods are allies only. Asuras and rakshasas oppose the player. All new strings must pass the 05.7 reverence lint.
- **No Pixi at module scope.** Simulation must run headless: Pixi is touched only behind the `SPRITES`/`VFX._px` guards.
- **Colours that get alpha suffixes must be full `#rrggbb`** (root cause of the freeze-after-first-boss bug, 589c817). The smoke `ctxStub` now throws on invalid gradient colours.
- **GSD workflow enforcement:** edits go through `/gsd:execute-phase`.

## Summary

Combat is concentrated in four files: `js/entities.js` (1546 lines: damage pipeline, Player, Enemy with an 8-case `switch (this.def.ai)`, Boss with the telegraph invariant), `js/game.js` (loop, projectile loops, hazards, rendering, HUD, indicators), `js/rooms.js` (wave composition, spawn, doors, room flow) and `js/renderer.js` (CAMERA math). Content is data-driven in `js/data.js`. The phase touches all of them, so the main planning problem is **sequencing work in shared files**, not choosing libraries. No new runtime dependency is needed or allowed.

Reading the code turned up four problems the planner must handle first:
1. **Boss telegraphs on the sprite path are incomplete.** Boss atlases ship, so real play uses `renderSpriteExtras` (`js/game.js:853-869`), which draws only `line` and `circle` telegraphs. Cone telegraphs draw as a full circle, `ring` telegraphs draw nothing (`tg.r` is undefined), and **every world-anchored `marks` telegraph is never drawn**. That covers lamps, crossBeams, ledger, noose, teleport, summon and quake. The smoke harness calls `B.draw()` directly, so it cannot see this. Any new boss or mini-boss move would hit the same gap.
2. **"Elite" today means an expensive unit type, not a modifier**, and the elite budget often cannot afford one. The smoke info lines show `elites(last)=0.00` for Naraka and Patala. Naraka has no unit costing 3 or more, and Patala's elite slice (~2.3) is smaller than the vetala's cost of 3. Most units are weak fodder (60-100%), half-HP pretas with plain chase AI. That largely explains "too easy and predictable".
3. **All gameplay RNG goes through `U`** (`js/utils.js`; 0 `Math.random` calls in entities/rooms/game). A seedable `U` therefore makes the balance sim deterministic with a one-file change. The smoke sandbox shares the host `Math` object, so do not patch `Math.random` there.
4. **The camera has no base zoom.** `zoom` is only the kill-punch pulse. Adding a base zoom changes `clampScroll`, `desiredScroll` and `xform`, and deliberately breaks two existing tests that assert rest rooms use the identity transform (`test/smoke.js:2148` and `:3315`; D-19 overrides that 05.1 decision).

**Primary recommendation:** Build a foundation plan first: seeded `U`, one shared telegraph renderer used by both draw paths, a Player status system with a `takeDamage` that returns whether the hit landed, data-driven projectile behaviours behind a backward-compatible `enemyShot`, an enemy-side telegraph invariant that mirrors `BOSS_TG`, and a bot-driven balance harness with a **baseline captured before any tuning**. Then layer player kit → AI and elites → new enemies → mini-bosses → bosses → camera → Heat → balance pass, with the GPU-bound art plan running independently. Gameplay already falls back to primitives when an atlas is missing (D-11), so no gameplay plan has to wait for art.

### Pattern 1: Player status system (STATUS) with a "hit landed" contract
**What:** `Player.takeDamage(amount, fromX, fromY, opts)` returns `true` only when damage actually applied. It returns `false` on iframes, dash, godMode or Lila dodge. Statuses and knockback are applied **only if it returned true**, so dash i-frames avoid statuses for free (D-11).
**When:** every enemy, projectile, hazard and boss hit that carries a status.
**Example:**
```javascript
// js/entities.js Player.takeDamage — today returns undefined in every branch (L469-509)
takeDamage(amount, fromX, fromY, opts) {
  if (this.iframes > 0 || this.dashT > 0) return false;
  if (GAME.godMode) return false;
  if (/* lila dodge */) { ...; return false; }
  ...existing damage...
  if (opts && opts.status) STATUS.apply(this, opts.status, fromX, fromY);   // stun/slow/root/burn/poison/knock/pull
  if (this.hp <= 0) GAME.onPlayerDeath();
  return true;
}
// DoT ticks must NOT go through takeDamage (see Pitfall 2):
tickDamage(amount, src) { if (GAME.godMode) return; this.hp -= amount; dmgText(...); if (this.hp <= 0) GAME.onPlayerDeath(); }
```
Status state: `P.st = { stunT, stunImmT, stunDR, slowT, slowF, rootT, burns:[], poison:[], kx, ky }`. Merge the existing `hazardSlowT/hazardSlowF` (venom pool, `js/game.js:470`) into `slowT/slowF`, or keep it and take `min()` in `moveSpeed()`.
Stun DR: duration × `0.5^(stuns in the last 4 s)`. After a stun ends, `stunImmT = 1.2 s`, during which any stun becomes a slow. The cap of 0.6 s is enforced in `STATUS.apply`.
Root: `rootT > 0` blocks movement (not attacks); `tryDash()` clears `rootT`.
Stun: blocks movement, attack, dash and cast input. Input handlers call `P.tryDash()` directly from keydown and pad edges (`js/game.js:68`, `js/gamepad.js:419`), so the guard must live **inside** `tryDash/tryCast/trySpecial/meleeAttack/updateBow`, not in the input layer.

### Pattern 2: Data-driven projectile families (PROJ behaviour table)
**What:** `DATA.PROJECTILES[id]` holds pure data (r, speed, colour `#rrggbb`, trail preset, hitFx preset, sfx, behaviour id, status, destroyable, pierceWalls). `PROJ.BEHAVIORS[behavior] = { spawn, update(p, dt), hits(p, P), draw(ctx, p) }` lives in code (`js/combat.js`). `enemyShot(x,y,vx,vy,dmg,color,r)` stays backward compatible: it maps to family `'bolt'`, which reproduces today's generic orb exactly. That keeps `killEnemy`'s bhuta ring (`js/entities.js:139`) and `Boss.shoot` working.
**Recommended roster (10):**

| Family | Behaviour | Tell / readability | Status variant |
|--------|-----------|--------------------|----------------|
| `bolt` (legacy) | straight | today's look | none |
| `needle` | straight, fast (520-600 px/s), r 3, volleys of 3-5 | 0.3 s muzzle glint on shooter | none |
| `orb` | straight, slow (120-160 px/s), r 16-22, ignores dash-destroy | big, slow, obvious | knockback on hit |
| `wisp` | homing, turn rate ~2 rad/s, life 3.5 s, max 6 alive per shooter | flickering trail | slow |
| `bomb` | travels then fuses 0.8 s (pulsing ring), splits into 6 needles | fuse ring | none |
| `beam` | line from shooter: 0.7-1.0 s tell line → 0.25 s active | thin line → thick flash | stun (Ranabhoomi sniper) or burn |
| `mortar` | lobbed: target circle on ground fills over 0.9-1.1 s, resolves at landing | ground circle (reuse `dangerZones` style) | burn pool (Naraka) / poison pool (Patala) |
| `chakram` | boomerang: out 0.6 s, returns to thrower, can hit twice (hit-set reset on turn) | spinning blade + trail | none |

### Pattern 4: AI coordination (ENEMY_AI): slots + attack tokens + range bands
**What:** a per-frame coordinator in `js/ai.js`, called once from `GAME.update` before the enemy loop:
- **Slots:** 8-12 angular slots on two rings around the player (melee ring ~60-90 px plus `e.r`, ranged ring ~260-380 px). Integrate by replacing the movement lines inside each `case` of `Enemy.update` (`js/entities.js:627-748`) with `ENEMY_AI.steer(this, dt)`, while keeping each AI's attack state machine. Keep `DATA.WAVES.engageRange` gating (05.1 WAVE-04) because tests at `test/smoke.js:3660-3730` assert it.

### Pattern 9: Camera base zoom + context zoom (exact math)
Let `Z` = eased base zoom, `p` = punch pulse (`zoomPulse()`, 1 when idle), `z = Z·p`, `S` = world top-left of the **unpunched** view, `F` = punch focus, `k` = kick + shake.
```
screen = z·(world − F) + Z·(F − S) + k
tz = Z·p
tx = Z·(F.x − S.x) − z·F.x + k.x          // with Z = 1 this is exactly 05.1 D-06 (regression-safe)
ty = Z·(F.y − S.y) − z·F.y + k.y
clampScroll:   hiX = R.x + R.w − VW/Z ,  hiY = R.y + R.h − VH/Z   (center when lo > hi)
desiredScroll: S = P + lookAhead − anchor/Z                      // anchor stays in screen px
```
- New CAMERA fields: `Z` (current), `ZT` (target), `CFG.ZOOM = { combat: 1.3, boss: 1.15, large: 1.2, rest: 1.4 }` and `CFG.ZOOM_RATE ≈ 3/s`. Ease `Z` in `CAMERA.update(dt)` (real dt, like punch). `snap()` also snaps `Z = ZT`, so room changes never zoom-swoop. `_followTarget()` returning null (hub, no player) must force `Z = 1` so the hub stays identity (asserted at `test/smoke.js:3083`).
- Pass `Z` explicitly: `clampScroll(dx, dy, R, z = this.Z)`. Existing pure tests then keep passing when `CAMERA.Z = 1`, and new tests cover 1.3.
- At 1.3 the view is 984.6 × 553.8 world px. Combat world rect 2480 × 1610 (arena 2240 × 1300 + pad) scrolls freely. Boss world rect 1840 × 1270 at 1.15 shows 1113 × 626, fine. **Rest world rect 1280 × 720 at 1.4 shows 914 × 514 and now scrolls.** That deliberately breaks `test/smoke.js:2148` ("sarovar room … camera transform is exactly identity") and `:3315` ("legacy (rest) arena … converges to exact identity"). Rewrite them to assert the clamp at `ZOOM.rest` instead. Never let `Z < 1` (the world rect would be smaller than the view and blank margins would show).
- `viewRect`, `visible`, `edgePoint`, `screenToWorld` already use `tz`, so culling, indicators, aim, GFX lights (`js/gfx.js:909-940`) and ENV culling (`js/environment.js:164`) follow automatically.
- Look-ahead: keep the small 261019-nnu values (`LOOK_AIM 0.06`, `MAX 40`, `MOVE 20`). The user previously reported "excessive camera motion". To cover threats, add a capped **threat bias** (≤60 px toward the centroid of armed/winding enemies within 900 px, slow rate) and new indicator kinds: `threat` (winding-up enemy off-view, pulsing) and `astra` (lodged cast ammo off-view).

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- **Applying statuses before checking the hit landed.** It breaks dash i-frames (D-11).
- **Routing DoT ticks through `takeDamage`.** Each call grants 0.5 s iframes plus flash and hitstop (`js/entities.js:482-490`), so poison would make the player invulnerable.
- **Putting a mini-boss in `GAME.bossRef`.** It advances the biome on death.
- **Calling `CAMERA.visible()` from AI.** It reads the last *rendered* transform, and headless `GAME.render()` early-returns (`js/game.js:622`), so the transform is stale in every smoke sim. Use the pure `viewBand(P)`.
- **Duplicating telegraph drawing** in `Enemy.draw` and `renderSpriteExtras`. Use the shared renderer.
- **Shortening tells through Hasty, Heat `haste` or timing variance.** Apply speed multipliers to movement and cooldowns, never below the ENEMY_TG/BOSS_TG minimums.
- **Short colours** (`#fff`) anywhere an alpha suffix may be appended (auras, rings, door glows).
- **Gating gameplay on art.** Primitive fallback (`js/game.js:820`) is the contract. Art lands independently.

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Sprite-path telegraphs silently missing (EXISTING BUG)
**What goes wrong:** players never see lamps, crossBeams, ledger bands, noose, teleport destination, summon points or the quake ring. Cones render as circles and rings render as nothing.
**Why:** `renderSpriteExtras` (`js/game.js:853-869`) mirrors only `line` and `circle`, and never draws `e.marks`. Boss atlases exist on disk, so the sprite path is the live path.
**How to avoid:** Pattern 7 in the foundation plan. Add a headless test with `SPRITES.has` forced true.
**Warning signs:** a boss move that "hits from nowhere" in playtest; `telegraph.png` from `browser-check --stress` shows no marks.

### Pitfall 2: DoT and statuses granting free invulnerability
**What goes wrong:** burn or poison ticks call `takeDamage` → `iframes = 0.5` each tick, so the player can't be hit while poisoned.
**How to avoid:** `P.tickDamage()` with no iframes, no hitstop and no flash. Use a small pip pulse instead, rate-limited.
**Warning signs:** a balance sim shows damage taken *falling* when poison enemies are added.

### Pitfall 3: Stale camera in headless sims
**What goes wrong:** AI "on-screen" checks, or the new threat bias, read `CAMERA.tx/ty/tz`, which `GAME.update` never refreshes headless (`render()` returns early without `SPRITES.bridgeCtx`; `follow()` runs only from `frame()`).
**How to avoid:** use pure helpers (`viewBand(P)`), or call `CAMERA.follow(dt)` explicitly inside new sims.

### Pitfall 4: Mini-boss side effects through `e.boss`
**What goes wrong:** `e.boss` disables knockback (`meleeAttack` L381), changes hitstop and kill VFX (`killEnemy` L131-136), is skipped by hazards (`updateHazards` L459), drops 8-12 coins, and makes `simulateRun` count 8 bosses.
**How to avoid:** decide each branch explicitly with `e.mini`. Recommended: no knockback, boss-style hitstop, 4-6 coins, hazards still hit them, exclude from `log.bosses`.

### Pitfall 5: Tuning-coupled 05.1 assertions break on purpose
**Assertions that encode today's numbers** (each needs a deliberate, documented update rather than a silent loosening):
- `naraka room1: mean units >= 15, weak% >= 0.6, exactly 3 waves` (`test/smoke.js:3462`)
- `naraka room1 elite < 0.5` and `ranabhoomi last elite >= 2` (`:3478-3479`); these count `def.cost >= eliteCost`, so switch them to `u.elite` once Pattern 5 lands
- threat ramps room1 < last, and vaitarani room1 > naraka room1 (`:3468-3476`)
- `Enemy mods: weak … dmg 0.6` (`:3500`)
- camera: rest-room identity (`:2148`, `:3315`); clamp `[0,1060]×[0,720]` holds only at Z = 1 (`:2952-3000`)
- `fights all 4 bosses` / `bosses.size === 4` (`:713`, `:1259`, `:2333`)
- `boss phase 1/2` loops (`:1433`, `:1490`) if a Heat phase 3 is added

### Pitfall 6: META whitelist load/save
**What goes wrong:** `META.load()` copies an explicit field list (`js/meta.js:24-37`), and `save()` and `wipe()` do the same. New `heat`/`bestHeat` fields silently vanish on reload if any one of the three is missed.
**How to avoid:** add the fields to all three. Add a round-trip smoke check like the existing meta check (`test/smoke.js:756-771`).

### Pitfall 7: Projectile explosions (perf)
**What goes wrong:** homing wisps, splitting bombs, Heat `storms` and 26 alive enemies grow `GAME.eprojectiles` unbounded. There is no cap today, and each projectile also emits trail particles.
**How to avoid:** `EPROJ_CAP ≈ 260`, a per-shooter cap for wisps (6), trail emission every other frame, and `VFX.count()` for reduced particles. Budget: keep `browser-check --stress` fps_avg ≥ 58 and p95 ≤ 20 ms on the dev Mac (05.6 baseline method).

### Pitfall 8: Hold-to-charge breaks auto-swing muscle memory
**What goes wrong:** melee currently auto-repeats while held. A charge-on-hold design changes that feel.
**How to avoid:** a 0.28 s delay before charging; a tap combo still flows. Call it out in the plan summary for user playtest. Alternative if the user dislikes it: charge on **special hold** instead (an open question).

### Pitfall 9: Art generation time blows the schedule
**What goes wrong:** the 05.7.4 board recorded **12-58 min per Flux image** under memory pressure (swap ~17.5/18 GB). 12 characters × up to 5 attempts could take 12-60 GPU-hours.
**How to avoid:** a separate art plan with no gameplay dependents; restart ComfyUI before each batch; serialize; generate at 768² (finished to 128/256 px anyway); cap at 2-3 attempts and mark the least-bad `FLAGGED:` per the README protocol; procedural animation needs only one idle key pose (+ a `phase2` variant for mini-bosses via `phase2_variant.py`). Provenance kind: use `enemy` and `boss`. Unknown kinds would skip the required-field check in `tools/check-provenance.js`.

### Pitfall 10: Reverence lint traps in new data
**What goes wrong:** the lint deep-walks all of `DATA`, `PHASE2_TITLES` and index.html text. A Heat description like "Yama's trial … bosses gain moves", or any sentence pairing a deity name with "defeat/slain", fails.
**How to avoid:** keep deity names out of Heat, mini-boss and projectile strings. Yama-related copy uses trial/judgment words only.

### Pitfall 11: Stun-lock and fairness
**How to avoid:** a 0.6 s stun cap, DR, 1.2 s immunity, and a ≥0.5 s stun wind-up. Stun never stacks with knockback into a hazard in the same hit (apply one of the two). Add an AI test: under a 30 s barrage from 6 stun sources, the share of time stunned stays ≤ 25%.

### Pitfall 12: Input executes outside `update()`
**What goes wrong:** keydown and pad edges call `tryDash/tryCast/trySpecial` directly (`js/game.js:68-70`, `js/gamepad.js:418-421`), including during hitstop.
**How to avoid:** status guards and dash-strike windows live inside the Player methods and use sim-time counters advanced in `update()`.
