<objective>
Build the Node-side asset tooling that turns ComfyUI's loose frame output into game-ready PixiJS atlases and guarantees every shipped atlas is traceable. `tools/pack-atlas.js` wraps `free-tex-packer-core` and INJECTS the `animations` block that free-tex-packer omits (RESEARCH Pitfall 1 — the single highest-risk technical detail of the phase). `tools/check-provenance.js` enforces ART-06: every `*-atlas.png` must have a matching `PROVENANCE.json` entry. The provenance ledger and the draft Steam AI disclosure (QA-04) are created here, ready for the art plans to append to.

Purpose: This tooling is the bridge between AI generation (Plans 04-06) and the runtime loader (Plan 02). It can be built and tested with synthetic placeholder frames before any real art or ComfyUI exists (RESEARCH §Environment Availability). Runs parallel to the renderer migration.
Output: `tools/pack-atlas.js`, `tools/check-provenance.js`, `assets/sprites/PROVENANCE.json`, `assets/sprites/AI-DISCLOSURE.md`, extended `test/smoke.js`.
</objective>
