**finish_env.py (new, importable + CLI).** Import rembg helpers from finish_sprite.py.
- `tile --in RAW --out OUT --size 1024 [--grade-limits]`: center-crop to square, resize, `make_seamless(axis='xy')`, grade, save RGB.
  - make_seamless: roll the image by half on the axis/axes. Build a feathered weight mask: 1 at the center, falling to 0 at the edges, cosine ramp, feather = 22% of the dimension. out = original·mask + rolled·(1−mask). The edges then come from the rolled copy, which wraps continuously, and the center comes from the original, which is continuous there.
- `strip --in RAW --out OUT --crop x0,y0,x1,y1 --width 1024`: crop the wall band, resize to width 1024 keeping aspect, then `make_seamless(axis='x')`. Also paste the result into a 3x1 preview when `--preview` is given.
- `prop --in RAW --out OUT --max 384|448|512 [--grade prop|architecture] [--silhouette SIL]`: rembg cutout → clean_alpha → despeckle → trim → fit into a max×max box keeping aspect. Unlike finish_sprite's square output, output the trimmed size (not padded to a square) with a 4px margin, and bottom-aligned semantics documented. Apply the HSV grade (sat×, val×) to RGB only.
- `decal --in RAW --out OUT --max 384 --max-alpha 0.55 [--grade decal]`: estimate the background colour from the median of a 24px border ring. alpha = clamp((ΔE_rgb − t0)/(t1 − t0)) with t0=10, t1=60 on a 0-255 scale, Gaussian-feathered (σ=2), scaled by max-alpha. Trim to the alpha bbox and fit to max.
- Every mode prints a JSON line: `{"mode","out","size","luma_mean","luma_std","sat_mean","grading"}`. env_build records this as provenance `grading`/`finish`.
- Grading is deterministic.
  - Tile: if the stats exceed `tile_limits`, apply contrast compression toward the mean (factor = min(1, limit_std/std)), a value scale to bring the mean luma ≤ the limit, and a saturation scale to ≤ the limit. Report `graded: true` and the factors.
