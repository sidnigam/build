## Phase Boundary

The six boon-granting gods are Hanuman, Agni, Indra, Vayu, Krishna and Durga. This phase gives each of them:
1. an illustrated, reverent **portrait**, and
2. a **voice**: data-driven, contextual dialogue shown on the boon-pick screen.

It also adds a small **gods section to the codex** (THE POTHI).

Out of scope for this phase:
- new boons or balance changes
- voice acting or audio
- dialogue anywhere other than the boon screen and the codex (for example the hub, bosses, or Narada)
- portraits for bosses, Narada or Chitragupta
- gamepad focus navigation (Phase 05.2 owns it; see the hooks below)

## Implementation Decisions

### Art: portraits (Plan 01)
- **D-01 Composition.** Each god gets one **half-body / bust** portrait, three-quarter view, facing slightly toward the viewer's right. The portrait sits on the left side of the screen and looks toward the cards.
  - Generation size: **896x1152**, an SDXL-native bucket. The orchestrator suggested 768x1024, but SDXL degrades below about 1MP, so 896x1152 is the refinement.
  - Finished size: **600x768 RGBA PNG** at `assets/sprites/gods/{id}-portrait.png`. It is trimmed, fitted with the bust cut at the bottom edge, and the bottom 12% of alpha is feathered so the bust fades into the panel.
  - Display size: at most about 520 CSS px tall on the 1280x720 stage. 768px of source keeps it crisp at 1.5x (1080p).
- **D-02 Style.** The look is the existing locked painterly, Hades-adjacent style: bold ink linework, strong silhouette. It is pushed further for portraits:
  - richer rendering and luminous rim light
  - ornate traditional Indian jewelry and textiles
  - classical Indian temple-mural and Pahari-miniature influences
  - **No living-artist names in prompts.**
  - The background is plain flat pale grey so rembg can cut it out cleanly.
  - There is **no generated halo disc**: past runs showed halos wreck the cutout (see the Yama lesson). The divine aura (prabhamandala) is drawn in CSS behind the portrait, tinted with the god's color, so it stays consistent and correct.
- **D-03 Iconography is a hard gate** (CLAUDE.md: mythological reverence). The per-god prompt requirements, negatives and self-review checklist are specified in the Iconography Bible below. Any image that fails a hard (H) check is **never shipped**, whatever the retry count.
- **D-04 Retries.** Up to **7 attempts per god** (`base_seed + 0..6`, `max_retries_god: 6`). Gods are the most-viewed art in the game, so they get more retries than enemies (5 attempts).
  - Every rejected seed and its one-line reason is recorded.
  - If no attempt passes every H check, the portrait file is **not shipped**. The UI falls back to the symbol, and the failure is flagged in the SUMMARY.
  - If H passes but a soft (S) check fails, ship the least-bad attempt with `self_review` prefixed `FLAGGED:`.
- **D-05 Seeds.** Reserved range: **71000–71599**. Base seeds: hanuman 71000, agni 71100, indra 71200, vayu 71300, krishna 71400, durga 71500. They do not collide with 41xxx (biomes), 52xxx (bosses) or 63xxx (enemies). Phase 05.4 must pick a different block.
- **D-06 Checkpoint swap is a config change.**
  - `tools/art/prompts.json` gains top-level `"checkpoint"` (filename) and `"checkpoint_label"` (provenance label, including the license), plus an optional per-subject `"checkpoint"` override.
  - `gen_subject.py` passes the checkpoint to `build_txt2img_graph(ckpt=...)` and records `params.checkpoint` and `params.checkpoint_label` in the sidecar.
  - `record_provenance.py` prefers the sidecar's `checkpoint_label` and falls back to the existing constant, so old sidecars stay valid.
  - If Phase 05.4 has already added equivalent keys, reuse its key names and skip that sub-step. Grep first.
  - The current value stays `sd_xl_base_1.0.safetensors`, the only checkpoint installed. Do not block on the 05.4 checkpoint research. If the orchestrator later swaps the checkpoint, the portraits can be regenerated with no code change.
  - Any new checkpoint must have a commercial-use license recorded in `checkpoint_label`.
- **D-07 `god` kind.** `gen_subject.py` gains `kind: "god"`:
  - positive = `{subject}, {palette}, {god_style_positive}`
  - negative = `god_style_negative + god_negative_extra + entry.negative_extra`
  - size `god_size`, output directory `gods`
  - It **must not** append `enemy_negative_extra`, which bans multiple arms, crowns and deity markers and would break Durga, Hanuman's crown, and more. Branch on `kind`, not on the key name.
- **D-08 Finishing.** A new `tools/art/finish_portrait.py` does:
  1. rembg u2net cutout
  2. alpha clean (<16 → 0)
  3. despeckle (keep components ≥2% of the largest, so Vayu's scarves and Agni's flames survive)
  4. trim
  5. fit into 600x768 (12px top margin, bottom-aligned, crop allowed)
  6. bottom feather
  7. optional `--mirror`
  8. optional `--silhouette`
  - **Never mirror Durga** (abhaya mudra is the right hand) **or Krishna** (flute handedness). Regenerate instead.
- **D-09 Provenance.**
  - `record_provenance.py --kind` accepts `god`. For `god` entries it adds `cultural_review: "pending — human cultural reviewer (QA-03)"`.
  - `check-provenance.js` matches `/-(atlas|bg|portrait)\.png$/` and adds `god` to the completeness-checked kinds.
  - Sidecars go to `assets/sprites/_raw/gods/{id}/{id}-portrait.gen.json` (committed). Raw generations and rejects go to `assets/sprites/_gen/gods/` (git-ignored).
- **D-10 Review sheet.** A new `tools/art/gods_sheet.py` writes `assets/sprites/_review/gods-contact-sheet.png`. For each god it shows: the portrait over a dark panel with a god-color aura (an in-UI mock), the silhouette, a 64px circle thumbnail, and a label (id, seed, rejected count, FLAGGED). PROVENANCE gets a top-level `review_sheet_gods`. The existing `contact_sheet.py` is untouched, which avoids re-laying out a working tool.
- **D-11 AI-DISCLOSURE.md** gets one appended section describing the god portraits: txt2img, fixed seeds, up to 7 attempts, rembg cutout, CSS-drawn aura, and that human cultural review is pending.

### Dialogue (Plan 02)
- **D-12 Location.** The engine and content live in the new file `js/dialogue.js` (global `const DIALOGUE`). Nothing goes into `data.js`.
  - `DATA.GODS[id].greet` stays as it is and is reused as each god's first generic line, so the pre-phase fallback text is still used.
  - Script order in `index.html`: `js/dialogue.js` goes immediately after `js/meta.js`.
  - `test/smoke.js` is append-only. Its appended blocks load `js/dialogue.js` into the sandbox themselves (`vm.runInContext(fs.readFileSync(...))`). Every `ui.js` use is guarded with `typeof DIALOGUE !== 'undefined'`, so the earlier full-run sims keep exercising the no-dialogue fallback path.
- **D-13 Line schema.**
  - Single-god line: `{ id, god, text, reply?, when?, once?, w?, pri?, chance? }`
  - Co-voiced duo line: `{ id, duo: '<duo boon id>', beats: [{s, t}, ...], w? }`
  - `DIALOGUE.beats(line)` normalizes every line to `[{s: speakerId, t: text}]`. Speaker ids are the god ids plus `'abhimanyu'`.
  - Text is rendered with `textContent` only, never innerHTML. Lines carry no wrapping quote marks.
- **D-14 Conditions** (all AND). `DIALOGUE.KNOWN_WHEN` is:
  - `first` (bool)
  - `minMeetings` (n)
  - `firstThisRun` (bool)
  - `lowHp` (bool, hpFrac ≤ 0.35)
  - `biome` (biome id)
  - `justDefeated` (boss id or `'*'`)
  - `holds` (another god id)
  - `lastDeathBiome` (biome id or `'*'`)
  - `lastDeathBoss` (boss id or `'*'`)
  - `lastRunWon` (bool)
  - `minDeaths` (n)
  - `minWins` (n)
  - `weapon` (weapon id)
  - `duo` (duo id; set on duo lines through the top-level `duo` field)

  Context derivation (`DIALOGUE.buildContext(godId, {game, meta, duo})`, which defaults to the globals):
  - `justDefeated` = `DATA.BIOMES[biomeIdx-1].boss` when `biomeIdx > 0 && roomIdx <= 1`. That is the first boon room after a boss.
  - `lastDeath*` and `lastRunWon` are populated only when `mem.lastRun.run === meta.runs - 1` (the previous run) **and** this is the god's first meeting in this run.
  - `holds` counts come from `player.allBoons()`. A duo boon counts for both of its gods.
- **D-15 Selection** (`DIALOGUE.select(godId, ctx, mem, rand = Math.random)`, pure):
  1. **Eligible lines:**
     - lines where `line.god === godId` and `when` matches, or
     - duo lines where `line.duo === ctx.duo` and `godId` is one of that duo's gods.
     - Lines marked `once` that are already in `mem.seen` are excluded.
  2. **Remove recent lines:** drop `mem.last[godId]` and `mem.lastId`. If that leaves nothing, fall back to the full eligible set. This guarantees no back-to-back repeat whenever an alternative exists.
  3. **Priority.** Take the line's `pri` if it is set explicitly. Otherwise derive it from the highest-ranked key in its `when`:
     - duo 90, first 100, justDefeated 70
     - lastDeath*/lastRunWon 60, lowHp 50, holds 40, weapon 35, biome 30
     - minWins/minDeaths/minMeetings 20
     - no keys (generic) 0
  4. **Tier walk.** Walk the tiers from highest to lowest. In each tier, keep the candidates that pass `rand() < chance`. Default chance by tier:
     - first, duo, justDefeated: 1
     - lastDeath: 0.8, lowHp: 0.8, holds: 0.5, weapon: 0.5, biome: 0.4, milestone: 0.35
     - generic: 1

     The first non-empty tier wins. Pick within it by weighted random, with weight `w / (1 + seen[id])`. This makes unseen lines favored.
  5. Return `null` only if the god has no lines at all.

  Recording is a separate step: `DIALOGUE.record(line, godId, mem)` increments `met[godId]` and `seen[id]`, sets `last[godId]` and `lastId`, adds the god to `runMet`, and calls `META.save()`.
- **D-16 Memory is kept in META** (a small surgical edit to `meta.js`), not in a separate STORE key. This keeps one save file, so Steam Auto-Cloud (Phase 6) syncs one path and `META.wipe()` resets it for free.
  - `META.dialogue` defaults to `{}`. `load()` restores it only if it is a plain object. `save()` writes it. `wipe()` resets it.
  - `DIALOGUE.mem(meta = META)` normalizes it in place to `{ met:{}, seen:{}, last:{}, lastId:null, runMet:{run:-1, gods:[]}, lastRun:null }`.
  - `DIALOGUE.noteRunEnd({result, biome, boss, run})` stores `lastRun`. `ui.js` calls it from `UI.death()` and `UI.victory()` in Plan 03.
- **D-17 Validation.**
  - `DIALOGUE.validate(lines = DIALOGUE.LINES)` returns an array of error strings. It checks:
    - unique ids
    - god, duo, biome, boss and weapon references exist
    - `when` keys are in KNOWN_WHEN
    - `holds` is not the speaking god
    - text is 1–240 chars and replies are ≤ 120 chars
    - there are no `<`, `>` or leading/trailing `"`
    - duo beats use only that duo's gods or `abhimanyu`
  - `DIALOGUE.coverage()` returns the missing-coverage errors, per GOD-04.
  - Smoke asserts that both return empty arrays.
- **D-18 Voice bible.** The tone is warm, wise and sometimes wry, in the Hades manner, but **always reverent**. The gods are powerful allies who love this boy.
  - **Krishna**: uncle and guide (Subhadra's brother). Playful, profound, quotes the Gita lightly.
  - **Hanuman**: protective elder. Humble devotion to Rama, rides Arjuna's banner.
  - **Durga**: fierce, serene mother. Carries the weapons the gods gave her.
  - **Indra**: proud king. He is **Abhimanyu's grandfather** (Arjuna's father). His pride is gently self-aware (Govardhan, Hanuman's jaw).
  - **Agni**: warm, hungry, the witness of oaths and mouth of the gods. He armed Arjuna and Krishna at Khandava.
  - **Vayu**: airy, calm, the breath of life. Father of Hanuman and Bhima.
  - **Abhimanyu's replies** are short, respectful and sixteen. He calls Krishna "Uncle" and Indra "Grandfather", and uses "-ji" or "deva" honorifics.
  - **The gods never threaten Abhimanyu, never mock the divine, and speak of Yama with respect.** They frame the Yama fight as a **trial or examination of dharma, not enmity**. This also gives in-fiction support to the open STATE.md concern about Yama as a boss.
- **D-19 Lore fidelity.**
  - Every factual reference in the content appears in the Lore Check table in Plan 02, with its source.
  - Popular or regional traditions (Abhimanyu learning in the womb, Panchamukhi Hanuman and the five lamps, the chariot burning after the war, the Durga Stotra in Bhishma Parva) are allowed, but they are labeled in the table as vulgate, regional or popular rather than critical edition.
  - Deliberately **avoided**: Sita's agni-pariksha, Kali or severed-head imagery, and any version in which Krishna causes Abhimanyu's death.

### UI (Plan 03)
- **D-20 Portrait presentation.** `#boonpick` gains:
  - `#god-portrait-wrap` (absolute, left 0, bottom 0, 34% width, 96% height, `pointer-events: none`), containing `#god-aura` (a CSS radial glow in the god's color plus a slowly rotating `--svg-mandala` mask) and `<img id="god-portrait">`
  - `#god-portrait-2`, a small circle inset for the partner god on duo offers
  - `#god-dialogue`, containing `#god-dialogue-lines` and a `#god-dialogue-skip` ✕ button

  When a portrait is known-loaded, `#boonpick` gets the `has-portrait` class. That class adds `padding-left: 34%`, narrows the cards to 220px and hides `.god-sym`.

  Animations:
  - entry: slide-in 0.45s from `translateX(-40px)` plus fade
  - idle: bob of 6px over 4s alternate; aura rotation 60s
  - Both idle animations are **gated behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`**.
- **D-21 Portrait loading.** Use a plain `<img>`; do **not** use SPRITES or Pixi, so there is no `sprites.js` change.
  - `UI.preloadGodPortraits()` creates one `document.createElement('img')` per god, once, and sets `onload` → `_portraitOk[id] = true` and `onerror` → `false`.
  - `presentGod` adds `has-portrait` only when `_portraitOk[id] === true`. Otherwise the symbol shows.
  - Headless: `onload` never fires, so the fallback path runs.
  - Path convention: `DIALOGUE.portraitPath(id)` → `'assets/sprites/gods/' + id + '-portrait.png'`.
- **D-22 Typewriter.**
  - It is driven **only by `setTimeout`**. The smoke sandbox has no `setInterval`, `performance` or `matchMedia`, and `setTimeout` there only queues.
  - Speed is 3 chars per 33ms tick, about 90 chars/s. There is a 350ms pause between beats.
  - A token guard (`UI._talkToken`) makes stale ticks no-ops after a skip or re-open.
  - Reduced motion (`window.matchMedia` present and matching) shows the text instantly.
  - While typing, `#boon-cards` gets the `awaiting` class (`opacity .4; pointer-events: none`).
  - Skipping completes all beats instantly and removes `awaiting`. The triggers are: a click anywhere on `#boonpick` (`panel.onclick` while busy), the ✕ button, or Enter, Space or Esc (a `keydown` listener bound once by `UI.bindGodDialogueKeys()`).
  - Card `onclick` handlers are **not** gated in JS. Smoke and 05.2 gamepad confirm still work.
- **D-23 Cross-phase hooks for 05.2** (gamepad focus nav): `UI.godDialogueBusy()` and `UI.skipGodDialogue()` are public. 05.2 should treat confirm-while-busy as skip.
- **D-24 ui.js edit discipline.** New behavior lives in **new self-contained methods**:
  - `preloadGodPortraits`, `presentGod`, `clearGodPresence`, `typeGodDialogue`, `skipGodDialogue`, `godDialogueBusy`, `bindGodDialogueKeys`, `noteGodRunEnd`, `renderCodexGods`

  Existing methods get only one-line hook calls:
  - `offerBoon`: call `presentGod`, and omit the old greet when DIALOGUE exists
  - `offerAmrit`: call `clearGodPresence`
  - `death` and `victory`: call `noteGodRunEnd`
  - `codex`: call `renderCodexGods`

  CSS goes in a **separate `<style id="gods-css">` block**, placed after `settings-css`.

  Headless-stub rules still apply: no `dataset`, `setAttribute`, `style.setProperty` or `querySelector`, and `style.cssText` is set before any other style property.
- **D-25 Codex.** `renderCodexGods(list)` is called right after `list.innerHTML = ''` in `UI.codex()`. It appends one `.codex-gods` block containing:
  - a heading
  - for met gods (`mem.met[id] > 0`): a 64px round thumbnail (`<img>`, hidden `onerror`), name, Devanagari, epithet (`DATA.GODS[id].title`) and `DIALOGUE.LORE[id]` (a `{s, d}` source and paragraph)
  - for unmet gods: a dim symbol and "Not yet met"

## Iconography Bible (prompt requirements + hard checks)

**Shared god style (`god_style_positive`):** "Hades-inspired illustrated character portrait, half-body bust from head to waist, three-quarter view turned slightly to the right, facing the viewer, painterly digital illustration with bold confident ink linework, richly detailed rendering, luminous warm rim lighting, divine serene presence, ornate traditional Indian gold jewelry and silk textiles, inspired by the Mahabharata and Ramayana and classical Indian temple murals and Pahari miniature painting, single figure, centered, head fully in frame with space above, plain flat solid pale grey background, high detail, concept art"

**Shared `god_style_negative`:** "pixel art, 3d render, photorealistic, photo, blurry, lowres, jpeg artifacts, deformed, bad anatomy, fused fingers, extra fingers, mutated hands, watermark, text, letters, signature, logo, caption, cropped head, out of frame, full body, legs, multiple characters, crowd, collage, character sheet, turnaround, busy background, scenery, landscape, frame, border, halo disc, circular background, sun disc, mandala background, western medieval armor, roman armor, greek toga, samurai armor"

**Shared reverence guard (`god_negative_extra`):** "sexualized, seductive, cleavage, revealing clothing, nudity, pin-up, demonic, demon, evil, menacing, snarl, fangs, horns, claws, glowing red eyes, skull, skulls, blood, gore, severed head, horror, creepy, zombie, grotesque, cartoon, chibi, caricature, meme, mocking, swastika symbol"
(The swastika is a legitimate sacred symbol. It is excluded only because it is easily misread by an international Steam audience; this is recorded as a deliberate, reviewable choice.)

**Lesson from Phase 2:** SDXL collapses "Hindu god" into a generic blue-skinned, four-armed, Vishnu-like figure (as Yama did). Each prompt therefore **leads with the god's distinguishing features**, and every non-Krishna, non-Durga god negates Vishnu markers ("blue skin, four arms, conch and discus, Vishnu").

**Self-review checklist (every attempt, with the Read tool, on both the raw image and the finished cutout):**
- H1: the correct deity, identifiable by the required attributes above
- H2: benevolent, serene or protective expression; no menace, fangs, horns, claws or red eyes
- H3: no sexualization; modest traditional attire; no pin-up pose
- H4: no horror or gore; no skulls, blood or severed heads
- H5: no conflated deity iconography (see the table)
- H6: no text, pseudo-script, watermark or swastika glyph
- H7: one primary figure (Durga's lion is allowed)
- H-god: the per-god hard checks in the table
- S1: bust composition, head fully in frame
- S2: hands acceptable at 240px (minor issues allowed, but they must be FLAGGED)
- S3: clean cutout (no background remnants or halo disc)
- S4: matches the house style, painterly and luminous
- S5: reads at a 64px thumbnail
