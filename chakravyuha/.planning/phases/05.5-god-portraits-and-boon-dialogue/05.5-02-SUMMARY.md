# Phase 05.5 Plan 02: God Dialogue Engine + Content Summary

**A pure, headless-testable dialogue engine (`js/dialogue.js`) with condition-gated priority selection, 81 reverent lines across the six boon gods plus 12 co-voiced duo lines, and persistent memory folded into the single META save.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~45 min
- **Tasks:** 2/2 completed
- **Files modified:** 4 (`js/dialogue.js` created; `js/meta.js`, `index.html`, `test/smoke.js` modified)

## Accomplishments

- Built `DIALOGUE` (global, `js/dialogue.js`): `KNOWN_WHEN`/`PRI`/`CHANCE` tables, `matches()`, `priOf()`, `select()` (priority-tier walk with chance gating, no back-to-back repeats, unseen-line weighting), `record()`, `buildContext()`, `mem()`, `noteRunEnd()`, `metGods()`, `validate()`, `coverage()`, `beats()`, `speakerName()`, `portraitPath()`, `duoGods()`. Zero DOM/timer access.
- Added `META.dialogue` as a surgical, four-point edit to `js/meta.js` (default, load, save, wipe) — one save file, ready for Steam Auto-Cloud later.
- Authored the full launch content: Hanuman (13), Agni (11), Indra (11), Vayu (10), Krishna (13), Durga (11) = 81 single-god lines, plus 12 co-voiced duo lines (2 per each of the 6 duo boons). Every god has a `once`+`first` line, ≥2 generic lines, a `lowHp` line, a `justDefeated` line, a `holds` line, a `lastDeath*`/`lastRunWon` line, a `biome`/`weapon` line, and ≥2 reply/abhimanyu-beat lines.
- Added `DIALOGUE.LORE[godId] = {s, d}` codex paragraphs for all 6 gods.
- `DIALOGUE.validate()` and `DIALOGUE.coverage()` both return `[]` against the real content.
- Appended two smoke blocks (engine, then content) with 14 new checks total — all green, including a 60-meeting-per-god simulation driven by the real `buildContext`/`select`/`record` pipeline and a reverence lint.

## Decisions Made

- Followed the plan's engine contract exactly (D-12 through D-19); no deviations to the selection algorithm, priority tables, or memory shape.
- `coverage()`'s "≥2 reply/abhimanyu-beat lines per god" rule counts both the god's own lines with a `reply` and any duo line featuring that god with an `abhimanyu` beat — implemented per the rule's literal wording even though every god already clears the bar from its own `reply` lines alone.
- The content lint (reverence guard) is implemented as plain Node regex over `JSON.stringify(DIALOGUE.LINES)` pulled out of the vm sandbox, rather than as a regex literal nested inside an `R(\`...\`)` template string — this sidesteps the escaping pitfall where `\b` inside an outer JS template literal is a backspace escape, not a literal backslash-b, before it ever reaches the inner vm parser.

## Deviations from Plan
